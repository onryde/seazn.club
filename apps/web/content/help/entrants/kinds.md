Every entrant is one of three kinds. This page shows you how to pick the kinds a division accepts, and how to change them later.

- **Team.** A named side plays each fixture. A team can carry a squad of players.
- **Individual.** One person plays each fixture. An individual holds at most one person.
- **Pair.** A duo plays each fixture. A pair holds at most two people.

Ask who is named on a fixture. If it reads "Riverside Rockets", you want teams, even if you also track which players appeared. If it reads "Asha and Ben", you want pairs.

## Pick the kinds when you add the division

In the new-division builder, the **Who plays** step has an **Entrants are** choice. What it offers depends on the sport:

| Sport | What the step offers |
|---|---|
| Football, cricket, hockey, ice hockey | Teams only |
| Chess | Individual players only |
| Badminton, tennis, table tennis, carrom | Individuals or Pairs |
| Volleyball | Teams or Pairs |
| Any other game | Teams, Individuals or Pairs |

The step tells you that you can change this later on the Entrants tab.

## Change the kinds later

Open the division, choose the **Entrants** tab and open the **Entrant kinds** block.

![The Entrant kinds block with Individual, Pair and Team chips, a Default kind, Squad numbers and Captain options, and the Save entrant settings button](/help/entrants/entrant-kinds.webp "The Entrant kinds block on a division that uses the sport's default")

1. Tick or untick the **Individual**, **Pair** and **Team** chips.
2. Choose the **Default kind**. The **Add entrant** form starts on it.
3. For teams, turn **Squad numbers** and **Captain** on or off.
4. Choose **Save entrant settings**.

**Reset to sport default** puts the block back to what the sport offers. This block is separate from the format, so it stays open after you start the division.

You cannot remove a kind while entrants of that kind are still active, or while registration entries of that kind are still waiting. The block says so. Withdraw or reject them first, then save again.

> **Example**
> A football division starts with Team only. You decide to add a pairs side event. Open **Entrant kinds**, tick **Pair** and choose **Save entrant settings**. The division now accepts both. Later you try to untick **Team** while the six teams are still registered. The save is refused until you withdraw them.

## Add an entrant of each kind

On the Entrants tab, **Add entrant** starts with a **Kind** choice, then **Name**, **Seed** and a **Player** picker. Pick people already in your directory, or type a name under **New player** and choose **Create player**. [Add entrants](/help/getting-started/add-entrants) walks through the whole form.

An individual with two people, or a pair with three, is refused. A pair with only one person is accepted, so you can enter a pair before the second player is known.

Clubs live in the Directory. See [clubs and teams](/help/directory/clubs-and-teams).
