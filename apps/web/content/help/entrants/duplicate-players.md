The same person often ends up in the directory twice. A merge folds the two records into one, and you can undo it.

- [Find possible duplicate players](/help/players/find-duplicates) covers the **Possible duplicates** panel and what it suggests.
- [Merge two players](/help/players/merge-players) covers the dialog, what moves, the public-settings rule and merging a pair by hand with **Merge…**.
- [Undo a merge](/help/players/undo-a-merge) covers **Recent merges** and what an undo puts back.
