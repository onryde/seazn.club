A **division** is a single draw inside your competition: "Men's A", "U14 Mixed", "Office Ping-Pong". Each division has one sport, one entrant kind (teams, individuals or pairs) and one format.

**Needs you** lists what is blocking the competition — a stage waiting on its draw, fixtures without a time, registrations to approve, and, for any division whose timetable has been published (even one you haven't pressed **Start** on yet), a stage with no fixtures yet (one that is seeded when the stage before it is completed, or one you never generated), a match in play with no scorer, or a result overdue — with one button each. The pill beside the title is derived from your divisions: **N in play** while matches are being scored, **Match day** when something is on today, the **next kick-off date** when one is scheduled, **Scheduled** when the competition is under way but nothing is dated yet, **Finished** when every division is complete, and **Setting up** only while nothing has been played at all. A division row shows its sport, one status line, and a red pill when something on it needs you.

## The division wizard

1. Open your competition and press **Add division**.
2. Pick the **sport** and variant — this decides how matches are scored.
3. Pick who plays: **teams**, **individuals** or **pairs**.
4. Choose a **format** — league, knockout, groups + knockout, and more. Not sure? Press *How this works* next to any format for a diagram and a live example, or read the [format guides](/help/formats).
5. Review and create. Nothing is locked until you start scoring, so experiment freely.

## Common questions

**Can I change the format later?** Yes, up until the division starts. After that the structure is locked so results stay fair.

**How many entrants can I add?** Per division: **64** on Community, **256** on Pro, unlimited on Enterprise. An [Event Pass](/help/billing/event-pass) lifts one competition on its own, and it comes in two sizes — **128** under the **M** size, **512** under **L**.

**How many divisions can a competition have?** **4** on Community, **10** under an **M** pass, **20** under an **L** pass, and as many as you like on Pro. Archiving a division gives its slot back only if that division never recorded a result — [archive and delete](/help/divisions/archive) has the detail. A division you set up wrongly and never played costs you nothing to throw away.

**The wizard refused my division — why?** It names which of two things happened. *"This competition is finished, so no new divisions can be added to it"* means the competition's status is **completed** or **archived**; set it back to Live if the event is still running, or start next season's competition ([finishing a competition](/help/getting-started/create-a-competition)). An upgrade panel instead means the **division limit** is full — and when archived divisions are what's filling it, the panel says so underneath ([archive and delete](/help/divisions/archive)).
