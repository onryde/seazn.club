When your fixtures look right, you start the division and score its matches. Scoring is free on every plan.

## Start the division

Press **Start tournament** at the top of the division. A dialog asks "Start this tournament?" and lists what changes. It says the timetable goes live to players, and what is still open: who can still join, whether the format can still change, and which match rules stay editable. Press **Start tournament** again to confirm, or **Cancel**. The badge changes from **Draft** to **Live**.

If the competition is still a draft, the dialog adds "Still a draft — not listed on your page or in search until you publish." and a link to its settings. See [create a competition](/help/getting-started/create-a-competition). Starting does not publish it. [A division's life](/help/divisions/lifecycle) shows what Start locks.

## Open a match and score it

1. On the **Fixtures** tab, press a match. Its page opens, headed **Match 1** and the two sides, with the state **scheduled**.
2. Press **Start match**. The state becomes **in play** and the pad wakes up. The **Activity** list gets its first entry, **Match started**.
3. Score it on the pad. Each sport has its own: tap a side to add a point or award a rally, or use **Enter final score** where the sport offers it. See [scoring basics](/help/scoring/basics) and [the sports](/help/sports/generic).
4. When the result is in, the match reads **decided** and names the winner. Press **Finalize result**. It then reads **finalized**, the pad goes away and **Match finalized** joins the **Activity** list. **Share on WhatsApp** sits beside **Finalize result** until you do.

![A match page for a generic sport: Asha Rao won 21 to 14, the state decided, and the Finalize result and Share on WhatsApp buttons.](/help/getting-started/match-decided.webp "A decided match, waiting for Finalize result")

With **Enter final score**, you set the home score and press **Confirm**, then the away score and press **Confirm**. The first press moves on to the away score, and **Back** returns you.

## What the standings do

Once a match is finalized, the **Standings** tab counts it. Players who have not played yet show 0 played. After scoring starts, the **Fixtures** tab says "Format is locked once scoring starts."

> **Example**
> A four-player league on the generic sport pays 3 points for a win and 0 for a loss. You enter 21 for Asha Rao and 14 for Dev Patel, then press **Finalize result**. **Standings** shows Asha Rao on 1 played, 1 won, 21 for, 14 against, a difference of 7 and 3 points. Dev Patel shows 1 played, 0 won, a difference of -7 and 0 points. The other two players still show 0 played.

## If you make a mistake

On a badminton pad, **Take back** undoes the latest tap. In **Activity**, each entry that still counts has a **Void** button, and **⟲ Void last entry** removes the latest one. Voiding does not erase the entry. The match is worked out again as if it never happened.

> **Example**
> On a badminton pad you award two rallies to Asha Rao and the score reads 2–0. **Take back** makes it 1–0. **⟲ Void last entry** then makes it 0–0. **Activity** keeps every entry, marks the two rallies **VOIDED** and adds two **Entry undone** rows.

A finalized match shows no **Void** buttons and no pad. [Fixing a mistake](/help/scoring/corrections) covers what you can still do, and the other sports.

## Who can score

- **Owners and admins** see the pad and **Start match**. A **Viewer** sees the match page and its **Activity**, with no pad and no **Start match**.
- **Officials** who accepted an assignment score their own matches. See [officials who score](/help/scoring/scorer-role).
- **A device link** lets a phone score one match with no account. See [device links](/help/scoring/device-links).

Under **Activity**, **Signed audit — paid plan** says the signed match audit trail download needs a paid plan or an Event Pass for the competition.
