A competition has a public page with scores, schedules, standings and entrants, and visitors read it on a phone with no account. This page shows you how to open it and send it.

## Check who can see it

Open the competition, then **Settings**. **Who can see this** says who can open the page:

- **Private**: the public address shows a not-found page to everyone, including signed-in members of your organisation. Members work on it in the console.
- **Link only**: anyone with the link.
- **Public**: anyone with the link, and your organisation page lists it once it is published.

A new competition starts as **Public** but is a draft, so it opens for anyone with the link and your organisation page does not list it yet. Set **Status** to **published** and press **Save changes** to list it.

A plan limits how many competitions can be **Public** or **Link only** once they are published or live. Community allows {{plan:dashboard.public.max:community}}, and a draft does not count. With no slot free, a new competition is created **Private** and the form says "This one will be created private". Publishing a **Public** or **Link only** competition when no slot is free is refused: **Save changes** shows "Your plan's public dashboards are all in use — archive a finished one, or upgrade." [Who can see what](/help/sharing/visibility) has the detail.

## Get the link

![A public competition page on a phone: the competition name and dates, the WhatsApp and Copy link buttons, the division and entrant counts, and the Overview, Matches, Table, Teams and Info tabs.](/help/getting-started/public-competition-page.webp "A competition's public page on a phone, as a visitor sees it")

- **Copy link.** In the competition's **Settings**, **Share link** shows the address and **Copy link** copies it.
- **WhatsApp and Copy link.** The public page has both under its title. **WhatsApp** starts a WhatsApp message with the competition name and the address in it.
- **View Public Page ↗.** Under **Open** in a division's header, and on the competition page, it opens the page for you to check.

A division has its own address. **View Public Page ↗** in a division's header opens that division's public page.

## Print a poster

**QR ↗** opens a printable PDF poster. Print it for the venue: scanning it opens the live page. A **Private** competition has no **QR ↗** and its poster address is a not-found page, so choose **Link only** or **Public** before you print.

## At the venue and on your own website

- **Slideshow ↗** opens a full-screen board for a TV. It needs you to be signed in, so open it from your console. See [the slideshow](/help/sharing/slideshow).
- **Present ▸** opens a board that anyone with the link can open. It sits under the competition title on a wide screen, and on the **Info** tab on a phone. See [presentation mode](/help/sharing/presentation-mode).
- To show standings or a schedule on your own website, see [embeds](/help/sharing/embeds).

> **Example**
> You create **Spring Open** as **Public**. It is a draft, so someone you send the link to can open it straight away, but your organisation page does not list it. You set **Status** to **published**, and your organisation page now lists Spring Open. You switch it to **Private**, and the same link opens a not-found page.
