Fixtures are the matches your format calls for. You make them from the division's **Fixtures** tab, once your entrants are in.

## Generate

1. Open the division and choose **Fixtures**.
2. On the stage card, press **Generate fixtures**. On a phone it is under **Stage tools**. Before you do, the card reads **Pending** and "No fixtures yet — generate them when entrants are registered." A knockout also says "Needs at least 4 entrants".

![The Fixtures tab of a league division just after Generate fixtures: the notice that 6 fixtures were generated, the stage card marked Active, and Round 1 to Round 3 with two matches each, every one showing No time yet and a Set time button.](/help/getting-started/league-fixtures-generated.webp "A four-entrant league just after Generate fixtures")

The stage turns **Active**. A notice says how many fixtures it made, and the matches are listed round by round. The matches show **No time yet** with a **Set time** button, and the tab counts them as **to schedule**.

> **Example**
> A league of four players makes **Generated 6 fixture(s) (0 already existed).** It is three rounds of two matches, so every pair plays once. A knockout of four makes two semi-finals and a final.

On a league the button then goes away, so you do not press it a second time. A Swiss works round by round: **Generate fixtures** lays out the rounds with empty places (**TBD** against **TBD**, marked **Awaiting draw**), and **Pair next round** seats the players of the next round.

## When the roster changes before you start

Add someone after you generated, and the stage card tells you, with the control your format needs. Each format in this table was seen on a division of four to six players before **Start tournament**.

| Format | The card says | You press |
|---|---|---|
| League | **Fixtures don't match the roster**, then "Active, but not on a fixture yet:" and the name | **Rebuild fixtures** |
| Knockout | The same notice, and **Update draw** appears beside **Complete stage** | **Update draw** |
| Swiss | **Waiting for the next round**, then "Not paired this round:" and the names of the players not yet seated | **Pair next round** |

**League.** **Rebuild fixtures** asks "Rebuild this stage's fixtures?" and says how many fixtures it deletes. It also says "Times and courts stay with each match's place in the draw." and "This can't be undone." If the fixtures carry officials, team sheets or linked scoring devices, the box lists them and says you will set them up again. Press **Rebuild** to go ahead, or **Cancel**.

> **Example**
> You generated a league of four, which made 6 fixtures. You add two more players. **Rebuild fixtures** warns that it deletes 6 fixtures. After **Rebuild**, the page says **Rebuilt — 6 fixture(s) replaced. 15 fixture(s) drawn in their place.** Six players make 15 matches in five rounds of three.

**Knockout.** **Update draw** puts a newcomer into a free bye if the bracket has one, and says "1 new entrant placed in a bye." If the bracket has no free place, it draws the bracket again and says "The bracket was drawn again to match the current entrants. Printed scorer sheets for this stage will stop working." A bracket that already has match setup on its lines, such as officials or team sheets, is not redrawn this way. **Rebuild fixtures**, beside it, lists what it clears first.

> **Example**
> You generated a knockout of five players, which left 4 matches to schedule. You add a sixth. **Update draw** says "1 new entrant placed in a bye." and the knockout now has 5 matches to schedule, two of them quarter-finals.

**Swiss.** Before round 1 is paired, the card lists every entrant after "Not paired this round:", so a newcomer is one more name in that list and **Pair next round** seats them with everyone else. Once round 1 is paired, a late player waits, and only then does the list name the latecomer alone. The card also says "Swiss pairs one round at a time. Use Pair next round to seat them." **Pair next round** refuses while the current round has undecided matches, so you seat the newcomer once round 1 has its results.

Once a match in the stage has a result, **Rebuild fixtures** refuses and says "Can't rebuild — this stage already has recorded results. Complete it as it stands, or use Generate to add missing entrants instead." On a league that has started, a player you add gets catch-up matches at once, with no **Rebuild fixtures** button to press. A player who arrives on their own waits on the Fixtures tab, and while no match in the stage has started you can choose **Rebuild fixtures** there instead of catch-up matches. [A division's life](/help/divisions/lifecycle) shows who can still join each format.

## Times and courts

Generated matches have an order but no clock. Each one has a **Set time** button, and the division has a **Schedule** page with the [schedule board](/help/scheduling/board). Times use your organisation's timezone. Until you set one, the tab says **Times shown in UTC**. See [timezones](/help/scheduling/timezones).
