Fixtures are the matches your format calls for. You make them from the division's **Fixtures** tab, once your entrants are in.

## Generate

1. Open the division and choose **Fixtures**.
2. On the stage card, press **Generate fixtures**. On a phone it is under **Stage tools**. Before you do, the card reads **Pending** and "No fixtures yet — generate them when entrants are registered." A knockout also says "Needs at least 4 entrants".

![The Fixtures tab of a league division just after Generate fixtures: the notice that 6 fixtures were generated, the stage card marked Active, and Round 1 to Round 3 with two matches each, every one showing No time yet and a Set time button.](/help/getting-started/league-fixtures-generated.webp "A four-entrant league just after Generate fixtures")

The stage turns **Active**. A notice says how many fixtures it made, and the matches are listed round by round. The matches show **No time yet** with a **Set time** button, and the tab counts them as **to schedule**.

> **Example**
> A league of four players makes **Generated 6 fixture(s) (0 already existed).** It is three rounds of two matches, so every pair plays once. A knockout of four makes two semi-finals and a final.

The button then goes away. You do not press it a second time.

## When the roster changes before you start

Add someone after you generated, and the stage card shows **Fixtures don't match the roster** and who is missing, such as "Active, but not on a fixture yet: Eva Lindqvist, Farid Haddad". Press **Rebuild fixtures**. It asks "Rebuild this stage's fixtures?" and says how many fixtures it deletes. It also says "Times and courts stay with each match's place in the draw." and "This can't be undone." Press **Rebuild** to go ahead, or **Cancel**.

> **Example**
> You generated a league of four, which made 6 fixtures. You add two more players. **Rebuild fixtures** warns that it deletes 6 fixtures. After **Rebuild**, the page says **Rebuilt — 6 fixture(s) replaced. 15 fixture(s) drawn in their place.** Six players make 15 matches in five rounds of three.

Once you press **Start tournament**, a league gives a late player catch-up matches and shows no **Rebuild fixtures** button. [A division's life](/help/divisions/lifecycle) shows who can still join each format.

## Times and courts

Generated matches have an order but no clock. Each one has a **Set time** button, and the division has a **Schedule** page with the [schedule board](/help/scheduling/board). Times use your organisation's timezone. Until you set one, the tab says **Times shown in UTC**. See [timezones](/help/scheduling/timezones).
