A package is one line on your sponsor price list: a name, a price, a tier and where it applies. When you add a deal to a sponsor, you pick a package. The package sets the sponsor's tier and the place its logo shows.

Open **Sponsors**, then the **Packages** tab. Owners and admins see it. A Viewer does not. The tab reads **Your price list. Pick a package when you add a deal to a sponsor: it sets their tier and where they show.**

![The Packages tab with four package cards, each showing a tier, a price, how many sponsors use it and a Retire button, and the What each tier gets list open below them.](/help/sharing/sponsors-packages.webp "The Packages tab with the tier list open.")

## Make a package

1. Select **Create package**.
2. Fill in **Package name** and **Price**, and choose a **Currency**: GBP, EUR or USD.
3. Choose a **Tier**: Title, Gold, Silver or Partner.
4. Choose a **Competition**. **Any competition** sells the package for your whole organisation. Pick one competition to sell a package for that competition only.
5. Tick **Includes On stream** if a deal on this package should put the sponsor on your stream too.
6. Select **Create package**.

The package appears as a card with its name, scope, tier and price. A card reads **Not used yet**, or **Used by 2 sponsors** once deals use it.

## What each tier gets

Select **What each tier gets** on the Packages tab to read this list on screen.

| Tier | Where it shows | Turns on stream |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Title** | Named under the competition name on your public pages. Also shows a 4:5 poster at the top of the competition page, when you add one. | 3 |
| **Gold** | A large logo panel on the sponsor board. | 2 |
| **Silver** | A smaller logo panel on the sponsor board. | 1 |
| **Partner** | Listed by name in the Partners line. | 1 |

A turn is one place in the stream rotation. A tier with more turns comes round more often. [The Sponsors hub](/help/sharing/sponsors-hub) explains the rotation.

## Retire a package

Select **Retire** on a card, then **Retire package**. Retiring a package stops new deals using it. Deals already on it carry on, and past deals keep its name and price. The tab then reads, for example, **Gold board is retired. New deals can't use it.** and **Show retired (1)** lists the retired packages.

## What your plan allows

On Community, every sponsor shows in one strip and takes one turn on stream. You can still make a Partner package for your whole organisation. A Gold, Silver or Title package, or a package for one competition, needs Pro or an Event Pass for that competition. An Event Pass covers its own competition only. The tab says so, with an **Upgrade your plan** link.

Online invoices are paid to your Stripe account. Seazn Club keeps a fixed platform fee on each sponsorship paid by card, on every plan. The Packages tab states the figure, and [Pricing](/pricing) lists the plans.

> **Example**
> You make **Gold board** at a price of 250 in GBP, tier **Gold**, for **Any competition**. The card reads **Not used yet**. You add a deal for **Quay Tyres** on it, and the card reads **Used by 1 sponsor**. Later you select **Retire**, then **Retire package**. **Quay Tyres** keeps its deal, and the next sponsor can't pick Gold board.
