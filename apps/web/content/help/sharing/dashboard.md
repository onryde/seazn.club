The public dashboard is your competition's home for everyone who is not running it: players checking when they play, parents following scores, the league watching the table. Its address starts with `/live/` and your organisation's name. It follows the competition's [visibility](/help/sharing/visibility).

![A division's public page at phone width, with the Schedule tab open, two finished matches in round 1, and the Present and Share on WhatsApp buttons beside the title.](/help/sharing/public-dashboard.webp "A division's public page on its Schedule tab.")

## What is on it

- **The competition page.** The competition name and dates, **WhatsApp** and **Copy link**, your sponsors, and the tabs **Overview**, **Matches**, **Table**, **Teams** and **Info**.
- **Division pages.** The schedule, the standings and the entrants, as tabs, and a bracket for a knockout. **Add to calendar** gives a calendar feed that you subscribe to once.
- **Match pages.** The score and its detail, with the sponsors block.
- **Share and Present.** **Share on WhatsApp** sits under the title on a division page, with **Present ▸**. See [presentation mode](/help/sharing/presentation-mode).

Every public page ends with **Powered by Seazn Club · Run your own free →**. See [growing your club](/help/sharing/grow-your-club).

## Your logo and brand colour

Your **Logo** shows on your public pages on every plan. Upload it in **Settings**, then **Organisation**: a PNG, JPG or WebP file, up to 2 MB.

**Brand color** colours your public pages and the TV board. You choose one of 11 swatches in the same place. A competition can override it with its own **Brand color** in the competition's settings. On Community the field reads **Brand color requires an upgrade** and the pages stay in the standard colours. Brand colour is on Pro and Enterprise. See [plans at a glance](/help/billing/plans).

An embed carries only the competition's own colour, not your organisation's. See [embeds](/help/sharing/embeds).

> **Example**
> You pick **Teal** as your organisation's **Brand color**. **Spring Open** has no colour of its own. Its competition page, division pages and **Present** board take the teal. You then give **Autumn Cup** its own **Brand color**. Autumn Cup's competition page uses that colour and Spring Open keeps teal.
