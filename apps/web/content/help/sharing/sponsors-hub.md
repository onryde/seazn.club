Open **Sponsors** in the top bar, or on your organisation's Competitions page. The hub has four tabs: **Overview**, **Sponsors**, **Packages** and **On stream**. Every member sees **Overview** and **Sponsors**. Only an owner or admin sees **Packages** and **On stream**. A Viewer who opens one of those two addresses lands on **Overview**.

![The On stream tab with the whole organisation selected, the rotation switched on at Normal, and a list of five sponsors with their tiers, none ticked yet.](/help/sharing/sponsors-on-stream.webp "The On stream tab with the rotation on and the sponsors list below it.")

Adding sponsors, packages and deals have their own pages: [sponsors](/help/sharing/sponsors), [packages](/help/sharing/sponsors-packages) and [deals](/help/sharing/sponsor-deals).

## On stream

**On stream** decides which sponsors appear on your match streams, and how often.

- **Applies to.** Pick **The whole organisation** or one competition. A competition with no setting of its own follows the organisation. Change anything on the tab and it gets its own.
- **Show sponsors on the stream.** The switch turns the rotation on or off for that scope. Before you set anything the tab reads **The rotation is off. Tick a sponsor below to switch it on at Normal.**
- **How often.** **Light** shows sponsors at the big breaks only. **Normal** reads **The big breaks, and every third small one.** **Busy** shows them at every break the sport has.
- **Where it shows.** **Small breaks show a lower third for 8 seconds. Big breaks show a banner for 15 seconds.**
- **Sponsors in the rotation.** Tick the sponsors that should air. On Pro, **Higher tiers take more turns.** A tick applies only to a sponsor with no live deal. A sponsor whose deal is live reads **Set by the live deal** and cannot be ticked here, and one that is not active reads **Not active**.

A package with **Includes On stream** puts its sponsor on the stream when its deal goes live.

## Overview

**Overview** shows each sponsor's numbers for a **Period**: **This year**, **Last 30 days**, or one competition.

- **Times shown** and **On screen** count the sponsor's cards on streams Seazn Club composes.
- **Link clicks** count taps on the sponsor's tracked links: the logos on your public pages.
- **Viewer impressions** reads **Coming soon**. It is not measured yet.

## Share report

An owner or admin sees **Share report** beside each sponsor on **Overview**, and **Report link** on the sponsor's own page. It makes a link that shows the sponsor's numbers for the dates you choose, to anyone who holds it, with no sign-in.

1. Choose **From** and **To**, then **Create link**.
2. Copy the link at once. It is shown only once. If you lose it, revoke it and make another.
3. **Active links** counts the links that still open. **Revoke** stops one at once.

## Archive a sponsor

A sponsor with deals, invoices or payments on record cannot be deleted. Archive it. Open the sponsor on the **Sponsors** tab and select **Archive**.

- An archived sponsor leaves your list, your public pages, the TV slideshow and the stream. Its deals, invoices, payments and clicks are kept.
- **Archive** is refused with **End or settle its deal first.** while a deal is live.
- Once you have an archived sponsor, **Show archived (1)** appears above the list. **Unarchive** on the sponsor's page brings it back.

> **Example**
> You switch **Show sponsors on the stream** on for **The whole organisation** and tick **Ridge Physio** and **Oakfield Dental**, who have no deal. **Quay Tyres** has a live Gold deal, so its row reads **Set by the live deal** and its place comes from its package's **Includes On stream**. You leave **How often** on **Normal**. Breaks differ by sport. In a football match, half time is a big break and a banner shows for 15 seconds. A small break takes a lower third for 8 seconds, and every third one carries a sponsor. Its Gold package includes On stream, so it takes 2 turns to each Partner's 1.
