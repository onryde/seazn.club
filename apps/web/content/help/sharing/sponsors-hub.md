Open **Sponsors** from your Dashboard. The hub has four tabs: **Overview**, **Sponsors**, **On stream** and **Packages and orders**. Every member can read Overview and Sponsors; only an owner or admin sees On stream and Packages and orders. Adding sponsors, tiers and packages are covered in [Sponsors](/help/sharing/sponsors).

## On stream

On stream decides which sponsors appear on your match streams, and how often.

- **Applies to.** Pick *The whole organisation*, or one competition. A competition with no setting of its own follows the organisation's; change anything on the tab to give it its own.
- **Show sponsors on the stream.** The switch turns the rotation on or off for the scope you picked. When no rotation has been set yet, ticking a sponsor switches the rotation on at Normal.
- **How often.** *Light* shows sponsors at the big breaks only, such as half time. *Normal* shows them at the big breaks and every third small one. *Busy* shows them at every break the sport has.
- **Where it shows.** A small break shows a lower third for 8 seconds; a big break shows a banner for 15 seconds. A sport with no natural breaks shows its sponsors on the scene screens instead.
- **Sponsors in the rotation.** Tick the sponsors that should air. When your plan has tiers, higher tiers take more turns. A sponsor that is not active cannot be newly ticked.
- **What it looks like.** For each sport in your divisions, the tab counts the breaks in your latest finished match and marks the ones that would get a sponsor card, so you can see what a setting does before a match starts.

## Overview

Overview shows each sponsor's numbers for a period: **This year**, **Last 30 days**, or one competition.

- **Times shown** and **On screen** count the sponsor's cards on streams Seazn composes. A card still on screen counts as shown, and adds its time once it ends.
- **Link clicks** count visits through the sponsor's tracked links: the logos on your public pages and the short link printed on a stream card. Automated previews and repeat taps within 30 seconds are not counted.
- **Viewer impressions** are not measured yet, so the column shows a dash.
- A deleted sponsor keeps its airings, but its link clicks are removed with it.

## Share report

An owner or admin sees a **Share report** button beside each sponsor on Overview. It creates a link that shows that sponsor's numbers for the dates you choose, to anyone who has it, without signing in.

1. Choose **From** and **To**, then **Create link**.
2. Copy the link straight away: it is shown only once. If it is lost, revoke it and create another.
3. **Active links** lists the sponsor's links that still open. **Revoke** stops a link opening at once.
