Every deal with a payment has an invoice. The sponsor opens it from a link, with no sign-in. You find the invoice number and a **Download invoice (PDF)** link on the deal's own page. See [sponsor deals](/help/sharing/sponsor-deals).

![An invoice page for a fictional sponsor, with the club name under From, the invoice number, the amount and a What's included list.](/help/sharing/sponsor-invoice.webp "The sponsor's invoice page, with the Download PDF and Print buttons.")

## What the sponsor sees

The invoice page shows:

- **From** the club, **Invoice** with its **Number** and **Date**, and **Billed to** the sponsor.
- A stamp for its state: **Paid**, **Unpaid**, **Refunded**, **Cancelled**, **Written off** or **Ended**.
- A **Description** line and the **Amount**, then the **Total**.
- **What's included**: the **Tier**, the **Competitions**, **Where you show** (**The club's public pages**) and **On the live stream** (**Yes** or **No**).
- **Download PDF** and **Print** buttons.

A state note sits under the stamp. A cancelled invoice reads **The club cancelled this sponsorship before it was paid. Nothing is due.** A refunded one reads **This payment was refunded in full.**

The invoice follows your organisation's **Public language**, the amount included. See [languages](/help/sharing/languages).

## How the sponsor pays

An online invoice has a **Pay by card** button. The sponsor pays on Stripe's secure payment page. An offline invoice has no button. When you have filled in **Bank details for offline invoices**, it shows **Bank details** and a line such as **Use SP-0001 as the payment reference**. Find the field under **Sponsor invoices** in **Settings**, on the **Connect** tab, next to **Address on invoices**, which prints on every invoice. Without bank details the invoice shows none, so tell the sponsor how to pay yourself.

## Send it, and send it again

A new deal makes its invoice at once. Nothing reaches the sponsor until you select **Send invoice** on the deal's row or page. **Resend invoice** sends it again.

Each send makes a new link. The old link stops opening and shows **This invoice link doesn't open anything**, with the line **It may be mistyped, or the club has sent a newer one. Ask the club for the latest link.** Ending a deal that is not yet paid also stops its payment link.

A second invoice for the same package asks **Send a second invoice?** first. A link already sent stays payable, so the sponsor could pay twice.

## Numbers

Invoice numbers run inside your organisation and look like **SP-0001**.

> **Example**
> You send **Ridge Physio** its invoice, then press **Resend invoice** because the first email went to the wrong address. The first link now reads **This invoice link doesn't open anything**. The new link opens the invoice, stamped **Unpaid**. The sponsor pulls out before paying and you select **End now**. The new link now reads **Cancelled** and **The club cancelled this sponsorship before it was paid. Nothing is due.**
