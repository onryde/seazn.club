The slideshow is a full-screen noticeboard for a TV or a projector at the venue. It cycles standings, results and the next matches by itself. You open it from your console, so the screen needs to be signed in to your organisation. For a board anyone can open with no sign-in, see [presentation mode](/help/sharing/presentation-mode).

![The slideshow showing a standings table on a dark board, with a Sponsors strip along the bottom and the counter 1 / 3.](/help/sharing/slideshow.webp "The slideshow on its standings slide, with the Sponsors strip.")

## Open it

Sign in as a member of the organisation, on the browser the TV uses.

- For one division: open the division, then the **Open** menu, then **Slideshow**.
- For a whole competition: open the competition, then its **Open** menu, then **Slideshow**. It rotates through every division that has something to show. Active divisions come first, and a finished division's final table still cycles.

The slideshow opens in a new tab. **Back** returns you to the competition.

## What it shows

Each slide stays for 9 seconds. A division's slideshow has:

- **Standings**, as a table.
- **Latest results**, with each match labelled by its round.
- **Coming up**.
- A bracket slide, for a knockout stage.
- **In play**, while matches are live. The rotation comes back to it every other slide.

A counter such as **1 / 3** shows where you are. A division with nothing to show reads **Nothing to show yet** and **Generate fixtures and this board comes alive.**

A **Sponsors** strip stays on screen under the slides. It is not a slide of its own. See [sponsors](/help/sharing/sponsors).

## Colour and live scores

On Pro, your organisation's brand colour tints the board. A competition's own **Brand color** takes priority. On Community the board has no colour. See [your public dashboard](/help/sharing/dashboard).

With the realtime scoreboard, a score appears the moment a scorer taps it, and the board checks again every 5 minutes as a safety net. The realtime scoreboard needs Pro, or an [Event Pass](/help/billing/event-pass) on that competition. Without it the board polls for new results about every 45 seconds, still cycles and still refreshes itself, so a venue screen is never blank.

## On a phone or tablet

A screen narrower than 1024 px shows **This board is made for a TV**, with **Open the live page** and **Show the board anyway**.

## Keep the TV awake

Turn the TV browser's screensaver off while it browses, or use an HDMI stick with a kiosk browser.

> **Example**
> A club runs **Spring Open** with two divisions, **Singles** active and **Doubles** finished. You open **Spring Open**, select **Open**, then **Slideshow**. The board starts with the Singles standings and moves on every 9 seconds to **Latest results** and **Coming up**, then Doubles. When a Singles match goes live, **In play** appears and the board returns to it every other slide.
