An Event Pass lifts one competition while it runs. Only an **owner** of the organisation can buy one. An admin sees the offer, but the upgrade page tells them **Only the organization owner can purchase upgrades.**

## Buy it

1. Open the competition. On its overview, choose **Event Pass — from** and the price. You can also start from **Plan & Billing**, under **Event Pass**: each competition that can take a pass has an **Upgrade — from** button.
2. The upgrade page shows the pass for this competition, its limits and the comparison with your plan. Choose **Buy the pass**.
3. Pay at the checkout.

![The top of a competition's upgrade page: the competition and organisation names, a line saying one payment upgrades this competition while it's running, the One-time panel with the price and the pass's division and entrant limits, and the Buy the pass button.](/help/billing/pass-buy.webp "The upgrade page for a competition on Community.")

When the payment goes through, the competition's overview shows **Event Pass active**, and the pass appears on **Plan & Billing** under **Event Pass purchases**. Its invoice is named after the competition, so several passes are easy to tell apart.

## Paying

- If you are the person who pays for the organisation's bill, the checkout can use the cards already saved on the bill. Any other owner enters a card at the checkout, and the bill's cards are never shown to them.
- Prices are in your billing currency. Your first purchase of any kind, a pass or a subscription, sets that currency for later purchases.
- If the same pass is paid for twice, for example by two people at once, the second payment is refunded automatically.

## When it is refused

- **The competition already has a pass.** You see **This competition already has an Event Pass on file. Passes are one-time and can't be bought again for the same competition.**
- **Your plan already covers it.** On Pro, the checkout is refused with **Your plan already includes everything this Event Pass adds.**
- **The competition is closed to passes.** See below.

## When a competition closes to passes

A competition can take a pass while it is running. It closes to new passes when:

- you mark it **completed** or archive it, or
- its end date passed more than 7 days ago.

The upgrade page then says **This competition is closed to Event Passes**, and the offer disappears from the overview and from **Plan & Billing**.

If the end date has passed but the event is still being played, the upgrade page offers **Update the end date →**. Correct the date, and the pass is offered again.

## Starting Pro after a pass

If you start Pro within 30 days of buying a pass, the pass comes off your first Pro invoice. This happens once per billing group, the first time it subscribes.

A competition with a pass cannot be deleted, because the pass is a payment record. Archive it instead. Nothing is lost.

> **Example**
> Spring Ladder's end date passed on 14 November, but the last round is still to play. On 22 November, 8 days later, the owner opens its upgrade page and sees **This competition is closed to Event Passes**. The owner follows the link, sets 6 December as the last day and saves. The upgrade page now shows **Buy the pass** again.
