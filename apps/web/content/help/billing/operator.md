Every organisation on one bill spends AI credits from the same wallet. The **Operator** panel shows that pool and how much each organisation has spent this month, and lets you cap any of them, so one busy club cannot use up the month's credits before the others. See [AI credits](/help/billing/credits) for what credits buy.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Plan & Billing** on an organisation on your bill. The **Operator** panel is there when you pay for the bill and it has more than one organisation on it. Nobody else sees it, and an organisation on a bill of its own has none. If you pay for several bills with more than one organisation, the panel appears on the one with the most organisations.

It shows:

- How many organisations share the wallet, and **Pool:**, the credits it holds right now.
- **Top up**, to buy more credits for the pool.
- A row for each organisation, with its **Monthly cap** and what it has **Used this month**.
- **Add organisation**, which takes you to **Organisations on this bill**. See [add an organisation to your bill](/help/billing/add-an-organisation).

## Set a monthly cap

1. On the organisation's row, press **Edit** beside its cap.
2. In the dialog headed **Allocate —** and the organisation's name, choose **Unlimited share of the pool**, or **Limit to** and type a whole number of credits.
3. Press **Save**.

![The Allocate dialog for Lakeside Tennis League, with Limit to chosen](/help/billing/operator-cap.webp "Setting a monthly cap of 4 credits for one organisation, with what it has used this period")

The editor shows **Used this period**, what the organisation has spent so far this month, and how many days are left until it resets. The new cap applies at once, and the row then shows the organisation's spend against its cap.

An organisation with no cap spends freely from the pool. That is how every organisation starts. An organisation with a cap can spend up to that many credits in a month. Once a run would take it over the cap, it cannot start that run until the month resets or you raise the cap, even when the pool still has credits. A run that fails and gives its credits back does not count toward the cap.

The month runs from the first day of each calendar month, counted in UTC. The cap limits what one organisation spends; it does not take credits out of the pool or set any aside.

## Top up the pool

**Top up** opens the credit packs, with each pack's price in your currency. A pack you buy here is for the whole bill: its credits land in the shared pool, and every organisation can spend them within its cap. See [buy a pack](/help/billing/credits#buy-a-pack) for how packs work.

> **Example**
> Alex Morgan pays for a Pro bill with Harbour Hockey Club, Lakeside Tennis League and Northfield Netball on it. Lakeside runs AI scheduling more than the others, so Alex presses **Edit** on Lakeside's row, chooses **Limit to**, types 4 and presses **Save**. Lakeside's row now reads 0 of 4. Lakeside can spend up to 4 credits this month. The other two still draw freely from the pool.
