An Event Pass lifts its competition while the competition is running. It stops applying when:

- you mark the competition **completed** or archive it, or
- the competition's end date was more than 7 days ago.

The 7 days are a grace week, so a competition that runs over its planned end keeps its pass through the finals.

## What you see

The competition's overview and its upgrade page show **Event Pass ended**, with the reason:

- **Nothing is deleted — this competition is finished or archived, so its Event Pass has stopped lifting its limits.**
- **Nothing is deleted — this competition is past its end date, so its Event Pass has stopped lifting its limits. Update the end date if it is still running.**

On **Plan & Billing**, the pass stays under **Event Pass purchases**, marked **Event Pass ended**, with its invoice.

The overview offers **Create the next edition** and **Go Pro — 14-day free trial**.

![The upgrade page of a finished competition: the Event Pass ended heading with the line saying nothing is deleted and the competition is finished or archived, and the pass panel marked Event Pass ended.](/help/billing/pass-ended.webp "A pass on a competition that has been marked completed.")

## What changes

- **Nothing is deleted.** Every entrant, result and page stays where it is.
- **The limits fall back to your plan's.** The extra divisions and entrants stay, but you cannot add more past your plan's limits. Branded exports, the realtime scoreboard and the sponsor tools stop for that competition.
- **The competition counts again.** If it is still active, it counts against your active-competition limit again. If that takes you over the limit, a competition becomes read-only. See [downgrading to Community](/help/billing/downgrade).
- **The entry-fee rate does not change** once the competition has taken a paid entry. See [the entry-fee rate locks when sales start](/help/billing/groups#the-entry-fee-rate-locks-when-sales-start).

## Getting it back

The reason is read from the competition each time, so correcting it brings the pass back:

- If the end date was wrong and the event is still being played, move the end date forward. The overview's notice **This competition's end date has passed** has a **Change the end date** button. The pass applies again.
- A finished competition stays finished. For the next one, choose **Create the next edition**. A new competition needs its own pass.

## When a pass is refunded

If a pass is refunded, or a card payment for it is disputed and lost, the pass is removed from the competition. The competition falls back to your plan's limits in the same way, and the owner gets an email. Nothing is deleted.

> **Example**
> Autumn Open holds an Event Pass, and its end date is Saturday 14 November. The finals overrun, but the pass keeps applying through Saturday 21 November. On Sunday 22 November the overview shows **Event Pass ended** with the end-date reason. The final is next weekend, so the owner chooses **Change the end date** and sets it to 29 November. The overview shows **Event Pass active** again.
