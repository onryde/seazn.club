When an organisation joins a bill you pay for, it runs on that bill's plan and is paid for on the same card and invoice. You need to be the person who pays for the bill and an owner of the organisation. An admin of the organisation cannot move it.

## When you create the organisation

1. On **Create an organization**, under **Billing**, choose **Add to an existing bill**.
2. The page lists the bills you pay for, each with its plan and how many organisations it holds. Pick one.
3. Press **Create & add to this bill**. When joining adds to a paid bill, the button names the amount instead, as **Create & add —** followed by the amount and "now". When nothing is added, as with a freed paid seat or a free trial, the button shows no amount.

A bill that cannot take another organisation is greyed out, with the reason beside it: **Payment overdue**, **Scheduled to cancel**, **Not active** or **Full**. A full Pro bill also offers **Buy another slot →**, which opens its **Add-ons** page. When no bill you pay for can take one, the page says "No eligible bills — each needs an open slot on Pro."

![The Billing choice on Create an organization, with Add to an existing bill selected](/help/billing/add-organisation-create.webp "Three bills to choose from: one Pro bill with room, one with a payment overdue, and a full Community bill")

That amount is added to your next invoice, even though the button says "now". Nothing is charged to your card when you create the organisation.

## Moving an organisation you already have

1. Open **Settings → Plan & Billing** on any organisation on the bill.
2. Under **Add an organisation**, choose the organisation to move.
3. Read the dialog, type **CONFIRM** and press **Move it onto this bill**.

The list shows the organisations on your other bills that have no paid subscription of their own. An organisation that pays for its own subscription is named with a line such as "Riverside Racquets pays for its own subscription, so it cannot join this bill yet. Cancel its subscription first — an organisation can only join from Community." When there is nothing to move, the panel says "You have no other organisations to move onto this bill. An organisation can only join from its own Community plan."

## What it costs

Nothing is charged to your card when an organisation joins. What the dialog says depends on the bill:

- **A paid bill.** Your bill goes up by the rate for one more organisation (see [what it costs](/help/billing/groups#what-it-costs)), prorated to the rest of the current period and added to your next invoice. When the amount can be worked out in advance, the dialog shows it. On a yearly bill the organisation renews on the bill's own date.
- **A paid seat is free.** If an organisation came off the bill outside a free trial and you chose **Free up the seat now**, the panel says, for example, "Paid slots free until renewal: 1. Moving an organisation into one costs nothing." Moving an organisation into that seat adds nothing to your next invoice. From the next renewal it is billed like the others. Taking off the last organisation on a bill ends the subscription instead, and leaves no seat.
- **A free trial.** The organisation rides your trial to the same end date. Nothing is charged, and it is first billed when the trial converts.

If a later invoice fails, the organisation stays on the bill and the usual rules for a failed payment apply to the whole bill. If it stays unpaid for 14 days, every organisation on the bill moves to Community together. See [when a payment fails](/help/billing/change-plan#when-a-payment-fails).

## When a bill cannot take one

- **The bill has an unpaid invoice.** Settle it first.
- **The bill is set to cancel at the end of its period.** Resume the subscription first.
- **The bill is full.** On Pro, the panel says "This plan covers {{plan:orgs.max_owned:pro}} organisations, and they are all in use. Buy an extra organisation from Settings → Add-ons — it's billed monthly on top of your current bill." An organisation that joins through an extra place is charged twice: as an organisation on the bill, and for the extra place. See [add-ons](/help/billing/add-ons).
- **The organisation pays for its own subscription.** Cancel it first, or wait for it to end.

## What changes for the organisation

It moves onto the bill's plan straight away: its limits, its features and its platform fee on card entry fees. A competition that has already taken a card payment keeps the rate it locked. Its own payouts and bank details are not affected, and its members stay as they were.

If it was the only organisation on its old bill, that bill closes and its AI credits come with it into the shared wallet. If other organisations stay on its old bill, the credits stay there with them.

> **Example**
> Alex Morgan pays for a Pro bill that is in its free trial. Alex opens **Plan & Billing** on Harbour Hockey Club and moves Lakeside Tennis League onto the bill. The dialog says Lakeside rides the free trial to the same end date and that nothing is charged. When the trial converts, Lakeside is billed with the others.
