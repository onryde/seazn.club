An **Event Pass** upgrades one competition while it is running. You pay once, and there is no subscription. It suits the event that comes round once or twice a year: the club championship, a fundraiser, a school sports day.

The price is on the [pricing page](/pricing) and on the competition's upgrade page.

The pass also adds, for that competition only:

- advanced formats, such as ladders and americano
- the realtime scoreboard and slideshow
- branded PDF and spreadsheet exports
- public player profiles
- sponsor tiers and online sponsor invoices

It also turns on career stats across its divisions, automatic officials assignment, automatic suspension tracking, embeds and auto-drafted result posts. The upgrade page shows the comparison above.

## What else you get

- **A free slot.** A competition with a pass does not count against your active-competition limit while the pass applies.
- **One-time credits.** Buying a pass adds AI credits and match credits to your organisation once. The amounts are on the [pricing page](/pricing). The AI credits never expire.

## What it does not include

- Your brand colour on public pages. That stays on Pro.
- API access.
- Anything outside this competition. Your other competitions stay on your plan.

## Where you see it

On Community, the pass is offered:

- on the competition's overview, as **Event Pass — from** with the price
- on the competition's settings page
- on **Plan & Billing**, under **Event Pass**, with a button for each competition that can take one

Once a competition holds a pass, its overview shows **Event Pass active**, and the pass is listed on **Plan & Billing** under **Event Pass purchases**.

On Pro and Enterprise, the pass is not offered. The upgrade page says **Your plan already includes every Event Pass feature, on every competition in** your organisation.

## One pass, one competition

A competition holds one pass, and it cannot be bought twice. A pass stays with its competition: you can rename the competition and the pass goes with it. A new competition, even the next edition of the same event, needs its own pass.

> **Example**
> Riverside Racquets is on Community. Autumn Open already has {{plan:divisions.per_competition.max:community}} divisions, and adding one more is refused. The owner buys an Event Pass for Autumn Open. Now the club can add divisions up to {{plan:divisions.per_competition.max:event_pass}}, and **Usage** on **Plan & Billing** counts one active competition fewer. Spring Ladder, in the same organisation, still stops at {{plan:divisions.per_competition.max:community}} divisions.
