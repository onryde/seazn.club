When the person who pays changes, the whole bill can change hands. Every organisation stays on it, on the same plan, so nobody has to take clubs off and add them back. The new payer brings their own card.

## Who you can hand it to

You can hand a bill only to someone who owns one of the organisations on it. Under **Settings → Plan & Billing**, **Hand this bill to someone else** lists those people by name, with the organisation they own.

If the person is not there, the panel says "You can only hand this bill to someone who owns one of the organisations on it. To hand it to somebody else, invite them into one of these organisations and make them its owner first — that is separate from billing." See [inviting your team](/help/getting-started/invite-your-team).

## Hand over a paid bill

On a paid bill, the panel says "They add their own card before anything moves. Your card stays on the bill until theirs is confirmed."

1. Under **Hand this bill to someone else**, choose the person.
2. Read the dialog, type **CONFIRM** and press **Offer the bill**.
3. The person gets an email, "You've been offered a billing group on Seazn Club", with a link. Their own **Plan & Billing** page also shows **A bill offered to you**.
4. They press **Take over this bill**, add their card and press **Confirm card and take over**.

![The Offer this bill to Sam Patel? dialog](/help/billing/hand-over-offer.webp "The offer: nothing changes until the person you choose adds their own card")

Until they confirm, nothing changes. You still pay, on your card, and you can **Withdraw** the offer at any time. An offer lapses on its own after 7 days, and a bill has one offer open at a time.

When they confirm, their card becomes the bill's card, and only then is yours removed. From then on you no longer manage the card or the plan.

## Hand over a bill with nothing to pay

On a bill with nothing being billed, such as Community or a cancelled subscription, there is no card step. The panel says "There is nothing to bill on this plan, so it moves as soon as you choose someone. No card is needed and there is no offer to withdraw."

Choose the person, type **CONFIRM** in the dialog and press **Offer the bill**. The bill is theirs at once, and they get an email, "You now pay for a billing group on Seazn Club". To get it back, you have to ask them.

## What happens to your invoices when the bill changes hands

Each payer sees only the invoices sent while they paid.

- **The new payer sees the invoices from the handover on.** They never see yours.
- **You keep yours.** On **Plan & Billing** of an organisation on the bill that you are a member of, **Your past invoices** lists them: "From when you paid for this group. Newer invoices belong to the current payer."
- **If the bill comes back to you later,** you see both of your own stretches, and still none of the invoices from whoever paid in between.

## What does not change

The organisations keep their plan, their limits, their entry-fee rates and their payouts. Each one keeps its own Stripe account and bank details. Only the payer, the card and who sees which invoices move.

> **Example**
> Alex Morgan has paid for a Pro bill for three clubs and is stepping down as treasurer. Sam Patel owns Meadow Cricket Club, one of the three. Alex offers the bill to Sam. Until Sam adds a card, Alex's card still pays. Sam opens **Plan & Billing**, takes over the bill and confirms a card. From then on Sam pays for all three clubs, and Alex's own **Plan & Billing** page says the clubs are covered by Sam Patel's subscription.
