The person who pays for a bill can take an organisation off it. The organisation then has a bill of its own, and its owner becomes its payer. Nothing is refunded, and nothing about the organisation is deleted.

The owner of an organisation on someone else's bill has no control for this on screen. Their **Plan & Billing** page says who pays. To get a bill of your own, ask that person to take your organisation off theirs.

## Take an organisation off

1. Open **Settings → Plan & Billing** on any organisation on the bill.
2. Under **Organisations on this bill**, press **Remove from this bill** on the organisation's row.
3. Choose what happens to its seat, if the dialog asks. See below.
4. Type **CONFIRM** and press **Take it off this bill**.

![The Take Oakridge Hockey off this bill? dialog on a paid bill](/help/billing/remove-organisation-dialog.webp "On a paid bill you choose between keeping its plan until the period ends and freeing up the seat now")

The dialog also says that competitions which have already taken a payment keep their locked fee rate, and that the organisation's payouts and bank details are unaffected, with its owner becoming its payer.

## Keep its plan, or free up the seat

On a paid bill the dialog asks, for example, "How should Oakridge Hockey leave this bill?" and you choose one:

- **Keep its plan until the period ends.** The organisation keeps the plan until the period you have already paid for runs out, then drops to Community. The paid seat leaves with it, so adding another organisation now costs a new seat.
- **Free up the seat now.** The organisation drops to Community straight away. After the free trial, you keep the seat you paid for, and the panel says, for example, "Paid slots free until renewal: 1. Moving an organisation into one costs nothing." Another organisation can move into that seat at no extra charge until the bill renews. During a free trial there is no paid seat to keep.

One seat cannot do both. If the bill has an unpaid invoice, the organisation moves to Community straight away, whichever you choose.

Taking off the last organisation on the bill ends the subscription at once. Nothing is refunded and no seat is left to reuse. You cannot take off the last one if you own it.

On a bill with nothing being billed, such as Community or a cancelled subscription, there is no choice to make. The dialog says, for example, "Oakridge Hockey moves to its own Community plan now, and its owner takes over its billing. Nothing is being charged for it on this bill, so there is nothing to refund."

At renewal, the bill charges for the organisations then on it.

If you do not own the organisation, you cannot put it back on your bill later: moving an organisation onto a bill needs the person who pays to be its owner.

## What the organisation keeps

- **Everything it holds.** Its competitions, members and settings stay. On Community, the usual limits apply. See [moving to Community](/help/billing/downgrade).
- **Its payouts.** Its Stripe account and bank details do not change.
- **Its locked fee rates.** A competition that has already taken a card payment keeps its rate. Competitions that have not use the new plan's rate.
- **No new trial.** The organisation's new bill carries over the old bill's trial record. If the old bill has ever been on Pro, the organisation cannot start a free trial.

Its AI credits stay with the bill it left, and it starts with an empty wallet of its own. If it was the last organisation on the bill, the credits go with it.

## Who hears about it

When the payer takes someone else's organisation off, its owner gets an email with a subject such as "Oakridge Hockey was moved off a shared bill". It says whether the organisation keeps the plan until a date or is on Community now, and that its billing is now theirs. When the payer also owns the organisation, nobody is emailed.

> **Example**
> Alex Morgan pays for a Pro bill with Pine Valley FC, Meadow Cricket Club and Oakridge Hockey on it. Oakridge's owner, Sam Patel, wants Oakridge on its own bill. Alex presses **Remove from this bill** on Oakridge and chooses **Keep its plan until the period ends**. Oakridge stays on Pro until the date Alex's period ends. Sam gets the email and becomes Oakridge's payer.
