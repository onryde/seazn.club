Streaming a match live from a phone spends one **Seazn credit**. It works on every plan. Without a credit, the match shows **You need a Seazn credit to go live.**

1 credit = 1 match stream, up to 3 hours.

## Open your Seazn credits

Open **Settings** and choose **Credits**. The page has two sides, switched at the top — **Streaming** and **AI**. It opens on **Streaming**, where you see:

- **Balance**: every Seazn credit the organisation can spend now.
- **Free this month**: how many of this month's free credits are used, and how many days until unused ones expire.
- **Bought credits**: credits from packs, marked *never expire*.
- **Buy Seazn credits**: the packs you can buy, with the price per match.
- **Credit history**: every grant, pack and streamed match, with the balance after each. **Export** downloads it as a spreadsheet file.

| Plan | Free Seazn credits a month |

| Community | {{plan:streaming.credits.monthly:community}} |
| Pro | {{plan:streaming.credits.monthly:pro}} |
| Enterprise | {{plan:streaming.credits.monthly:enterprise}} |

Free credits that are not used by the end of the month expire. The history shows them as **Unused free credits expired**.

An Event Pass adds a one-time batch of Seazn credits when it is bought. They show in the history as **Event Pass credits**. The number is on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Buy a pack

1. Under **Buy Seazn credits**, pick a pack. The middle one is marked **Most clubs**.
2. Pay. When the payment clears, the page says **Payment received. Your Seazn credits are in your balance.**

If the payment is still processing, the page says so, and the credits arrive as soon as it clears. Bought credits never expire.

## When a credit comes back

If a stream starts but the camera never connects, the credit is returned. The history shows **Refund: camera never connected**.

## Each organisation has its own

Seazn credits belong to one organisation. Organisations that share a bill still have their own balance and their own free credits. AI credits work differently: see [AI credits](/help/billing/credits).

> **Example**
> Riverside Racquets is on Community. On 22 November its Streaming side shows **Free this month** at 0 of {{plan:streaming.credits.monthly:community}} used, and the balance matches it. The club wants to stream three finals in December, so it buys the 5-credit pack. **Bought credits** now shows 5. November's free credits expire at the end of the month if they are not used, and December's arrive in their place. The 5 bought credits stay until they are spent.
