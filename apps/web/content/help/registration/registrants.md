The **Registrants** tab is your list of everyone who has entered your competition. Open your competition, choose **Registration**, then **Registrants**. It shows every entry across all divisions, with the player or team, the division, the **Status**, the **Payment** and when it was submitted.

## Find an entry

Search by name, email or reference. Filter by **Status**, **Division** and **Entrant kind**, or tick **Solo sign-ups** or **Consent pending**. Choose **Newest first** or **Oldest first**. The search and sort are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a view.

![The Registrants tab filtered to the waiting list](/help/registration/waitlist-queue.webp "The Registrants tab, filtered to Status: Waitlisted")

The **Status** filter has seven choices: **Pending**, **Paid**, **Confirmed**, **Waitlisted**, **Withdrawn**, **Expired** and **Rejected**. **Pending** means the entry is waiting for a fee, for your approval or for both. **Paid** means the money has arrived but the entry is not confirmed yet. That happens on a division with **Manual approval** when a player pays by card: the entry stays at **Paid** until you press **Approve**.

**Export CSV** downloads the entries the current filters show, one row per player. The `entry_reference` column holds each entry's own reference.

## Open an entry

Choose a row to open it. You see the contact details, the entry, the answers to your questions, the roster and any other entries in the same cart. A team entry also has a **Join link**. Give it to a captain who is still adding players. Anyone with the link can add players to that entry, so share it only with people you trust.

From a row you can:

- **Approve** or **Reject** an entry on a division with **Manual approval**. **Reject** asks you to confirm, refunds a paid entry and offers the place to the next person on the waiting list. **Approve** on a **Paid** entry confirms it. **Approve** on an entry whose fee is still due records your approval, and the entry stays **Pending** until it is paid.
- **Mark paid** when a player has paid you in cash or by bank transfer. On a division that **Auto-confirms**, the button in the confirmation reads **Mark paid & confirm** and the entry becomes confirmed. With **Manual approval** it reads **Mark paid**, the entry shows **Paid**, and it still needs your **Approve**.
- **Send payment reminder** to email a player whose fee is still owed. You can send up to 5 in any 24 hours for one entry.
- **Resend confirmation email** if a player lost theirs.
- **Withdraw** an entry. It asks you to confirm, frees the place for the waiting list and applies the [refund rules](/help/registration/card-payments#refunds).
- **Promote from waitlist** on a waitlisted entry. It does not check the division's capacity. See [the waitlist](/help/registration/waitlist).
- **Refund** a paid card entry. The entry keeps its place.

Which of these appear depends on the entry's status and how it is paid. Someone who can only view the competition sees every entry and the export, but no join link and no action buttons.

## Place solo sign-ups on teams

If a team division allows solo sign-ups, players without a team appear under **Solo sign-ups waiting for a team**. The line shows how many people are waiting and how many free places your teams have.

Open a solo sign-up that is paid or confirmed and choose **Assign to a team**. The list shows each team and how many of its places are filled, for example "0 of 1 places filled". Choose a team and the player joins it. **Remove from team** puts the player back in the pool. A solo sign-up still waiting after the **Place-by date** is withdrawn and refunded. See [open registration](/help/registration/open-registration#allow-solo-sign-ups).

> **Example**
> **Doubles Teams** has three teams, each with one free place. Isha Menon signs up solo and pays. Her row reads **Solo sign-up**, **Waiting for a team**. You open it, choose **Assign to a team** and pick **Harbour Hawks**. The row now reads "On Harbour Hawks", and that team has no free place left.
