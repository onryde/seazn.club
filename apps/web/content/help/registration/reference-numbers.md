Every registration gets a **reference** like `SZ-7F3K-Q2ND`. It appears on the player's registration page, on their ticket and in their emails. It lets a player check on their entry without an account. It also lets you find them on the day.

A reference uses letters and numbers chosen so they are hard to misread over the phone. It carries no personal information.

## One reference for each entry

A player can put several entries in one cart. The cart has one reference. When it holds two or more entries, each entry also gets its own, made from the cart's reference with the entry's number on the end, such as `SZ-7F3K-Q2ND-2`. Emails about one entry quote that entry's reference. It opens the same ticket as the cart's.

An entry keeps its number even if an earlier entry in the cart is later removed.

> **Example**
> Maya enters **Open Singles** and her team enters **Doubles Teams** in one cart. The cart reference is `SZ-7PTF-7J2S`. Her singles entry is `SZ-7PTF-7J2S-1` and the team entry is `SZ-7PTF-7J2S-2`. A bank transfer for the team entry would quote `SZ-7PTF-7J2S-2`.

## The ticket

Open `/r/` followed by the reference on the Seazn Club site, for example `/r/SZ-7F3K-Q2ND`. Capital letters do not matter. The page shows a ticket for the whole cart, with one stamp for each entry. A stamp reads Received, Paid, Confirmed, Waitlist, Withdrawn, Expired or Not accepted. The ticket also carries a QR code, a **View the live dashboard** button and a share button. The share button opens a phone's share sheet, or WhatsApp on a computer, with a message and a link to the competition.

![The top of a ticket with two entries, each with its own stamp](/help/registration/ticket.webp "The top of a player's ticket: one stamp per entry")

The ticket is for looking at. A player cannot withdraw from it. To pay a fee, resend the confirmation, invite teammates or withdraw, they use the registration page. Their confirmation email links to it. See [registering for a competition](/help/registration/registering-for-a-competition).

## On the day

Ask players for their reference. Search for it on the **Registrants** tab, where search also finds a cart by its reference and each entry by its own. See [managing registrants](/help/registration/registrants).

## Common questions

**A player lost their reference.** Search for their name or email on the **Registrants** tab. Open the entry and choose **Resend confirmation email**. The email carries the reference and the link to their registration page.
