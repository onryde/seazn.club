Set a **Capacity** on a division and new sign-ups stop at it. Once every place is taken, the next player is offered the waiting list instead of a refusal. When a place frees, the first player in line gets it. Your plan's entrant limit works the same way as a capacity.

Two things sit outside the queue. **Promote from waitlist** does not check the capacity, so promoting into a full division takes it over its capacity. A solo sign-up never joins the waiting list.

## What players see

A full division shows **Full · join the waiting list**, with the number already waiting in brackets, for example "Full · join the waiting list (3 waiting)". The button on its card reads **Join waiting list**.

![A full division on the sign-up page with a Join waiting list button](/help/registration/waitlist-card.webp "A full division, with two players already waiting")

A player who joins gets a reference like everyone else. Their registration page shows **Waitlisted** and their place in line, for example "You're #2 on the waiting list". It also says no fee is due unless they are promoted. They are not charged while they wait.

## When a place frees

When a confirmed entry is withdrawn, the first entry on the waiting list is promoted automatically. The player is told by email. What happens next depends on the division:

- A free division that **Auto-confirms**: the entry is confirmed at once.
- A division with an entry fee that **Auto-confirms**: the entry becomes **Pending** until the fee is paid. On a card division the player gets 48 hours to pay. See [how card entry fees flow](/help/registration/card-payments). On a pay-the-organiser division it stays pending until you mark it paid.
- A division with **Manual approval**: the entry stays pending until you approve it. On the player's registration page a free entry reads "Not confirmed yet: waiting for the organiser to approve this entry." An entry with a fee reads "Not confirmed yet: waiting for payment and for the organiser to approve this entry."

You can also promote someone yourself. Open **Registration**, choose **Registrants**, and set **Status** to **Waitlisted**. Each entry shows its place in line (#1, #2 and so on). Go by that number. The list is ordered by when entries were submitted and opens on **Newest first**, so #2 sits above #1. Choose **Oldest first** to turn it round. An entry that lost a promoted place goes to the back of the queue whatever its submission time. Open a row and choose **Promote from waitlist**.

> **Example**
> **Open Singles** has two places and both are taken. Tara Bose joins the waiting list and sees "You're #1 on the waiting list". Ravi Das joins after her and sees "You're #2 on the waiting list". Priya Nair, who holds a place, withdraws. **Open Singles** is free and auto-confirms, so Tara is promoted and her entry is confirmed at once. Ravi moves up to #1.

## Going back on the waiting list

A promoted entry on a card division that has not paid when its 48 hours end goes back on the waiting list. It joins the back of the queue, behind everyone already waiting, and the next player in line is offered the place.

## Common questions

**Do waitlisted players pay?** No. Money is taken only once a place is theirs.

**Does the waiting list count against capacity?** No. Only pending, paid and confirmed entries hold a place.

**Can a solo sign-up join the waiting list?** No. A solo sign-up is refused when the solo pool is full. See [allow solo sign-ups](/help/registration/open-registration#allow-solo-sign-ups).

**Can I promote someone into a full division?** Yes. **Promote from waitlist** does not check the capacity, so the division can end up with more entries than its capacity. The row then reads, for example, "3 of 2 registered".
