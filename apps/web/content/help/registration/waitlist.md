Set a **Capacity** on a division and it can never overfill. Once every place is taken, the next player is offered the waiting list instead of a refusal. When a place frees, the first player in line gets it.

## What players see

A full division shows **Full · join the waiting list**, with the number already waiting in brackets, for example "Full · join the waiting list (3 waiting)". The button on its card reads **Join waiting list**.

![A full division on the sign-up page with a Join waiting list button](/help/registration/waitlist-card.webp "A full division, with two players already waiting")

A player who joins gets a reference like everyone else. Their registration page shows **Waitlisted** and their place in line, for example "You're #2 on the waiting list". It also says no fee is due unless they are promoted. They are not charged while they wait.

## When a place frees

When a confirmed entry is withdrawn, the first entry on the waiting list is promoted automatically. The player is told by email.

If the division has an entry fee, the player pays after promotion. On a card division they get 48 hours to pay. See [how card entry fees flow](/help/registration/card-payments). On a pay-the-organiser division the entry stays pending until you mark it paid.

You can also promote someone yourself. Open **Registration**, choose **Registrants**, and set **Status** to **Waitlisted**. The list shows the whole queue, with each entry's place in line (#1, #2 and so on) in the order they joined. Open a row and choose **Promote from waitlist**.

> **Example**
> **Open Singles** has two places and both are taken. Tara Bose joins the waiting list and sees "You're #1 on the waiting list". Ravi Das joins after her and sees "You're #2 on the waiting list". Priya Nair, who holds a place, withdraws. Tara is promoted, and her entry is confirmed.

## Going back on the waiting list

A promoted entry on a card division that has not paid when its 48 hours end goes back on the waiting list. It joins the back of the queue, behind everyone already waiting, and the next player in line is offered the place.

## Common questions

**Do waitlisted players pay?** No. Money is taken only once a place is theirs.

**Does the waiting list count against capacity?** No. Only pending, paid and confirmed entries hold a place.
