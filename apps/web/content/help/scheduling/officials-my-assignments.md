Once an official [claims their profile](/help/scheduling/officials), they find their matches on their own home page, under **Officiating**. They use the same sign-in as for any player profile they hold.

![An official's home page. Two matches sit under My assignments, one marked Accepted with a Score this match link and one marked Declined with an Accept button. Below them are the Can't make these dates box and Download my rota.](/help/scheduling/officials-me.webp "An official's Officiating section: one accepted match, one declined, the dates they can't make, and Download my rota.")

## My assignments

Each match under **My assignments** shows:

- The two sides, and the role, for example **As Referee**.
- The competition, division, organisation and court.
- The time twice: in the venue's time zone and in the official's own, for example **19 Nov 2026, 13:23 GMT-7** and **20:23 GMT**.
- Where they stand: **Awaiting your response**, **Accepted** or **Declined**.

Matches already played go under **Completed matches**.

## Accept or decline

A match **Awaiting your response** has two buttons, **Accept** and **Decline**.

1. Select **Decline**.
2. Type a reason in the box, **Why can't you make it? (optional)**.
3. Select **Confirm decline**.

The organiser sees the reason when they hold the pointer over the **✗** chip on the [Officials tab](/help/scheduling/officials-assign).

Two rules limit changes:

- Once an official has accepted, they cannot decline it themselves. The match shows **Accepted** with no **Decline** button. They ask the organiser to release them.
- A declined match shows **Declined** and **Accept**. The official can accept it again until the match's scheduled start time.

## Dates they can't make

Under **Can't make these dates**, the official selects a date, adds an optional note and selects **Add date**. Each date shows as a chip with a **✕**. Select the **✕** to take it off again. The box says **Organisers see these dates when assigning matches.**

A date applies to every organisation the official works for. On the organiser's Officials tab it shows as **— unavailable** in the picker and a **⚠** on a match that day.

## Score a match

Once an official has accepted, **Score this match →** opens the match's page. They score from there with the same sign-in. No device link is needed.

## More than one organisation

Each organisation that invites an official has its own invite. When an invite is waiting for the email address the official is signed in with, **Pending invites** appears on their home page, with a line such as **Riverside Club set up an officiating profile for Marta Quinn.** and an **Accept** button. Accepting one does not touch the others.

## Download my rota

**Download my rota** gives a PDF of the official's own assignments, across every organisation they work for. It carries no organisation's branding.

> **Example**
> Marta Quinn claims her profile and lands on her home page. She sees two matches, both **Awaiting your response**: Birch Lane v Cedar Hill at **13:23 GMT-7**, which is **20:23 GMT**, and Cedar Hill v Amber Ward ten minutes later. She accepts the first. For the second she selects **Decline**, types "Travelling that day" and selects **Confirm decline**. The first now shows **Accepted** and **Score this match →**. The second shows **Declined** and **Accept**. She adds 19 November under **Can't make these dates**.
