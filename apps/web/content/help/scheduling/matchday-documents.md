The **Documents** menu gives you the paperwork for the day: a timetable for the venue, blank match sheets for the table, a rota for your officials and tickets for the gate. Each one downloads as a file. Nothing is emailed. Documents are free on every plan.

![The top of a printed Match sheets page, under the organisation masthead, showing the first sheet. It shows the two teams, Result and Notes lines, and signature lines for the referee and both captains.](/help/scheduling/matchday-documents.webp "A printed page of match sheets: result and notes lines, and a signature line for the referee and each captain.")

## Open the menu

Open the division, go to the **Fixtures** tab and select **Documents**. The menu lists eight documents:

| Document | PDF | XLSX |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Order of play** | yes | yes |
| **Match sheets** | yes | yes |
| **Officials rota** | yes | yes |
| **Admit tickets** | yes | no |
| **Bracket poster** | yes | no |
| **Team rosters** | yes | no |
| **Standings** | yes | no |
| **Participants** | no | yes |

Print the PDF. Use the XLSX when you want to rework the table in a spreadsheet.

## What each one holds

**Order of play** lists the division's matches with time, court, home, away and stage. It is listed by stage and round, not by clock time. A match with no time shows **TBD**.

**Match sheets** are one blank sheet per match, two to a page. Each has the two entrants, the time and court, a **Result** line, a **Notes** line, and signature lines for the referee and both captains. They suit scorers working on paper, or a backup when a device dies. For a sheet with a QR that opens the match on a phone, see [scorer sheets](/help/scoring/scorer-sheets).

**Officials rota** groups this division's assignments by official. It lists only matches still to play or in play. Each one lists when, court, competition and division, role and match, with signature lines for time on and time off.

**Admit tickets** print one ticket for each confirmed registration in the whole competition, two to a page. Each has a QR code that opens that registration's status page. Entrants you added yourself, without a registration, get no ticket.

**Bracket poster** prints the knockout tree, filled in from live results.

**Team rosters** list each team's squad, with columns for number, name, date of birth and signature, and lines for the team captain and an official.

**Standings** prints the table once a result is recorded. **Participants** is a spreadsheet of entrants and their players.

## When a document cannot print yet

Some rows tell you why, under the row:

- **Admit tickets**: **No confirmed registrations yet, so there are no tickets to print.**
- **Bracket poster**: **This division has no knockout bracket yet.**
- **Standings**: **No standings to print yet — they appear once a result is recorded.**
- Any document that fails: **Couldn't prepare that document.**

> **Example**
> A four-team league has six matches, three of them on a court. **Order of play** lists all six, with **TBD** on the two that have no time yet. **Match sheets** prints six sheets on three pages. **Officials rota** lists one referee and one match. **Bracket poster** shows **This division has no knockout bracket yet.** On the cup next to it, the same button downloads the poster, with the two semi-finals filled in and the final reading **Winner of R1·1** against **Winner of R1·2**.

## Branded and plain

On Pro and Enterprise, and for a competition with an [Event Pass](/help/billing/event-pass), each PDF carries your organisation's masthead: the **SEAZN CLUB** wordmark and your organisation's name. When the division has a logo and a colour set, the masthead uses them. When the competition has sponsors, each page footer carries a sponsor line grouped by tier. The XLSX files carry your organisation's name, and a Sponsors row when there are sponsors. On Community the same documents download as plain tables with the same data and layout, without the masthead, the sponsors and the logo.

The footer of the Order of play, Standings and Bracket poster also carries a small QR code to the division's live page, when the competition is public or unlisted.

## Your own rota

An official gets their own rota without console access. Their personal home has **Download my rota** in the **Officiating** section. It is a PDF of their own assignments, across every organisation they officiate for. See [what an official sees](/help/scheduling/officials-my-assignments).
