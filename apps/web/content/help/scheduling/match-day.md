**Match Day** is the page you keep open while the matches are on. It lists every court (or pitch, table or board — the word follows the sport) with what is happening on it: a match playing, a match **Called** and waiting for its players, a court **Free**, a court **Closed**, or a court **In use** by another competition that shares the venue. Pick a court to see its scoreboard, the matches queued after it and what could start there.

## Open it

On a day with matches, the competition page shows an **Open Match Day** button. On other days it is under **Open** (on a phone, **More**) as **Match Day**. Owners, admins and editors run the day; anyone else who can see the competition opens the same page read-only, with no way to call a match or make a scorer link.

## Call a match to a court

Pick a free court. It lists the matches that could start there in three groups: **Due here next**, **Waiting elsewhere** (other courts' queues) and **No court yet** (ready matches nobody has placed). Press **Call to** *court* on the one you want.

- A sheet shows what the call moves before you confirm it. Each later match on that court shows its planned time and the time it is now expected, marked with a **~**.
- **Planned times never move.** The call writes the court and time of the match you called, nothing else. The published schedule, the slideshow and your players keep seeing the planned times; the **~** estimates live only here.
- Players are not notified. Tell them where to go.
- The court shows **Called: waiting for players** until the scorer starts the pad. Called the wrong match, or nobody showed? **Undo call** puts it back on its old court at its old time.
- A match whose player is on another court is greyed out with the reason ("Zara Khan is on Court 3"). A court with a match playing or called takes no call, and a court another competition is using shows **In use**.
- A match pushed later, a short rest gap or a court running past its hours is a warning on the sheet, not a stop: you can still confirm.

## Closed courts and "Call anyway"

A court shows **Closed** when its venue's hours say it is shut — until a time, between two times, or for the rest of the day. You can still call a match onto it: the sheet says so first, and the button reads **Call anyway**.

## Filters

**Divisions** narrows the page to some divisions' matches. When your courts are spread over more than one venue, **Venues** does the same for venues. Both only change what is listed; they never stop a call.

## Remote scoring

On a playing or called court, **Remote scoring** shows a QR code and link for that match: scan it on any phone and that phone scores the match, no account needed ([device links](/help/scoring/device-links)). A court marked **No scorer** has nobody scoring yet. **Open pad** opens the match's own page.

## Common questions

**Why can't I call a match from Match Day?** Either you can only view the competition (the page says **View only**), or your organisation is over its plan's limit on active competitions. Over the limit, matches still score: **Open pad** works as usual, and so does the scorer link if your plan includes scorer links. What stops is calling or uncalling a match and connecting a new stream (a stream already running keeps going, and you can still stop it). Archive a competition or upgrade your plan to get those back ([plans](/help/billing/plans)).

**Where did "Needs you" go on a match day?** It moved here: Match Day has the list, behind a button in its header. The competition page keeps one line that links to it with the count, instead of listing the same rows twice.
