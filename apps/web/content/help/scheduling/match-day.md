**Match Day** is the page you keep open while the matches are on. It lists every court with what is happening on it, and lets you call the next match onto a free one.

![Match Day on a phone-width screen with six courts. One match is playing with No scorer, one court is Called, two are Free, and one is Closed for the rest of today.](/help/scheduling/match-day.webp "Match Day with a match playing, a court called, a free court and a closed court.")

## Open it

On a day with matches, the competition page shows **Open Match Day**. On other days it is the first item in the **Open** menu, as **Match Day**.

Owners, admins and editors run the day. Anyone else who can see the competition opens the same page and sees **View only. Calling matches and scorer links are for the competition's organisers.** They cannot call a match or make a scorer link.

## Read the court list

The header counts what is on: for example **6 courts**, **1 playing**, **4 free**. Each court row shows one of these:

- **Playing**: the match on it.
- **Called**: a match is waiting for its players.
- **Free**.
- **Closed**, with the hours it is shut.
- **In use: Other Cup**: a match from another competition is on this court. The panel reads **Another competition has a match on this court. It can't be used from here until that match ends.**

A free court with nothing queued folds into a line such as **2 free courts**. Select that line to open them. Select any court to see its scoreboard, the matches planned after the current one under **Up next here**, and what could start there.

## Call a match to a court

1. Select a free court. It lists the matches that could start there in three groups: **Due here next**, **Waiting elsewhere** and **No court yet**.
2. Select **Call to** and the court name on the match you want. A sheet opens.
3. Read the sheet, then select **Call to** again to confirm.

The sheet shows what the call moves. Each later match on that court shows the time it was planned and the time it is now expected, marked with a **~**: **planned 19:29, now ~19:49**. It always ends with **Players aren't notified. Tell them where to go.**

After you confirm, the court shows **Called: waiting for players** and **Called at** with the time. The scorer starts the pad when the players are on.

### What a call changes

A call moves the match you called, and nothing else. That match takes the court you chose and the time of the call, and the public schedule shows it there. Every other match keeps its planned time. The **~** times are estimates you see only on this page.

### Undo a call

Select **Undo call** to put the match back on its old court at its old time.

### What greys a match out

A match whose player is already on another court is greyed out with the reason, for example **Birch Lane is on Court 1**. A court with a match playing or called takes no call, and a called court lists no matches to call.

> **Example**
> A cup and a league share two halls. Court 2 is free and reads **3 matches could start here now**. Four more are greyed out because a player is on Court 1. You call the cup semi-final Garnet v Fenwick, planned for 20:34 on Court 1. The sheet shows one later match on Court 2, Birch Lane v Cedar Hill, planned 19:29 and now **~19:49**, and **1 match starts later than planned.** You confirm at 19:19. Court 2 shows **Called at 19:19**, and the semi-final's own time becomes 19:19. Every other match keeps its planned time. **Undo call** puts the semi-final back on Court 1 at 20:34.

## Closed courts and Call anyway

A court shows **Closed** when its own opening hours say it is shut. The row reads **Closed for the rest of today**, or a range such as **Closed 19:36–20:06**. You set opening hours and closed dates on each court, in the venue's courts.

You can still call a match onto a closed court. The sheet says so first, for example **Court 4 is closed for the rest of today. Call anyway?** The button reads **Call anyway**.

## Warnings on the sheet

A warning on the call sheet never stops the call. You can confirm anyway. The sheet warns you when:

- Later matches start late: **1 match starts later than planned.**
- The match would still be on when the court closes: **Court 3 closes from 19:36 to 20:06, while this match would still be on.**
- A player's rest is shorter than the division asks for.
- The court's matches would run past its closing time.

## Filters

**Divisions** narrows the page to some divisions' matches. **Venues** does the same for venues, and it appears only when your courts are in more than one venue. Both only change what is listed. They never stop a call.

## Scoring from Match Day

On a playing or called court, **Remote scoring** gives a QR code and a link for that match. Scan it on any phone and that phone scores the match, with no account. See [device links](/help/scoring/device-links). Select **Create scoring link** to make one. **Open pad** opens the match's own page.

A playing court shows **No scorer** while nobody has started scoring it, no official is assigned to it and it has no scoring link. The mark clears once a link exists.

## Stream a court

One phone on a tripod can stream every match on a court. See [streaming a court](/help/scheduling/match-day-stream).

## Common questions

**Why can't I call a match?** Either the page says **View only** because you can only view the competition, or your organisation is over its plan's limit on active competitions. In that case the page reads **Your organization is over its plan's limit on active competitions. Matches still score, but you can't call or uncall them here or connect a new stream. Archive a competition or upgrade your plan to run the whole day from this page.** See [plans](/help/billing/plans).

**Where is the Needs you list?** It is behind a button in the Match Day header, such as **3 need you**. The competition page keeps one line that links to it.
