After a match is decided, you can give each official who covered it a mark from 1 to 5. Marking is free on every plan.

![The Rate officials section of the Officials tab. Two decided matches each list the referee with five numbered tiles, and the tile for 4 is lit on the first match with a Clear button beside it.](/help/scheduling/official-marks.webp "Rate officials: five numbered tiles for each official, one tap to save, and Clear once a mark is set.")

## Give a mark

1. Open the division, then **Schedule**, then the **Officials** tab.
2. Find **Rate officials**. It lists the decided matches that had an accepted official, with the official's name and role.
3. Tap a tile from **1** to **5**.

One tap saves it. The lit tile is the mark. Tap another tile to change it. **Clear** appears once a mark is set, and it removes the mark. There is no box for a comment.

A match appears under **Rate officials** only when it is decided and its official **accepted**. An official who declined is not listed. A match still to play is not listed.

## Read an official's average

Open **Directory**, then **Officials**, and select **Marks** on a row. You see the average as a badge, such as **4.0** and **AVG · 3**, the count, such as **3 marks**, and the latest marks, newest first, each with its match. The list shows the last five. The average and the list count only your organisation's marks.

## What the official sees

The official sees only their own average, across every organisation that has marked them, on their home page under **Officiating**. They see it once they have three marks. Until then, if they have a completed match, the same spot reads **Collecting marks…**. They see the average only, not the individual marks.

## A mark stays when a result is voided

A mark rates how the official did, not the result. If you void a result, the match goes back to being played and drops out of **Rate officials**. The mark stays and still counts in the average. To remove a mark, use **Clear** before you void the result.

> **Example**
> Marta Quinn referees three matches. You give her a 4, then a 5, then a 3. After the second mark her home page reads **Collecting marks…**. After the third it shows **4.0** and **AVG · 3**. In the Directory her row shows **4.0**, **AVG · 3** and **3 marks**, with the 3 at the top of the list. You then clear the 4. Her row reads **AVG · 2** and **2 marks**, still **4.0**, and her own home page reads **Collecting marks…** again.
