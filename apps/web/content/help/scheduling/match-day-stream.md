One phone on a tripod can stream every match on a court, one YouTube video per match. You set it up once for the day, on **Match Day**.

## Link the court

1. On Match Day, select the court, then **Stream this court**.
2. Under **YouTube channel**, pick a connected channel. If you have none, use **Connect YouTube**. A court streams to a connected YouTube channel, one video per match.
3. The court gets a QR code. It reads **Scan with the Seazn Club app: Live Stream.** and **Works until 23:59 today**.

The code works until the end of the day. Select **Show court QR** to bring it back. A court streams to one channel a day: **This court already streams to another channel today.**

Once the court is linked, its row and its panel read **No phone yet**, then the channel's name, **Works until 23:59 today**, **Show court QR** and **Stop streaming** with the court's name. People who can only view the competition see the same state, without the buttons.

## Scan it on the phone

In the Seazn Club app, open **Live Stream** and scan the court's QR. The court's state then changes between **No phone yet**, **Phone waiting**, **Live** and **Phone offline**.

## Switch matches on

Every match on the court has a **Stream** switch: the one playing and each one under **Up next here**. All of them start off. Turn on the matches the phone should stream.

When the scorer starts a match whose switch is on, the phone starts that match's own video on the channel. About 30 seconds after the result, the video ends, and the court shows **Video ends in** a number of seconds. If the next switched-on match starts first, it takes over. The phone does not pick a match whose switch is off.

Each video needs a Seazn credit, the same as going live from the match's own page. Without one the match shows **You need a Seazn credit to go live.**

A match that has its own stream code shows **This match has its own stream code**, and its switch stays off until that code is ended.

> **Example**
> Court 1 has a league match playing and two more waiting under **Up next here**. You select **Stream this court** and pick **Harbour Club channel**. The court reads **No phone yet**, **Harbour Club channel** and **Works until 23:59 today**. The three **Stream** switches are all off. Turn on the ones you want the phone to stream.

## On the match page

A match streamed by the court's phone reads **Streamed by Court 1's phone** on its stream panel. It has no code of its own to scan.

Switching a match off while its video is on air ends that video at once, with no 30-second wait, and the court does not start that match again by itself.

## Stop streaming a court

Select **Stop streaming** with the court's name. The page asks first: **Unlink Court 1? Its phone stops following matches. A match on air finishes first.** After you confirm, the QR stops working.
