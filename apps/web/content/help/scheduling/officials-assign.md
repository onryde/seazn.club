You assign officials on a division's **Officials** tab. Every official in your [organisation's pool](/help/scheduling/officials) is on offer to every division. Picking an official by hand is free on every plan.

![The Officials tab of a division with the Auto-assign settings and a table of matches. Each row has the kick-off time, the assigned official with an accepted or declined mark, and a picker. Matches with no kick-off time show a dash.](/help/scheduling/officials-panel.webp "The Officials tab: the Auto-assign controls and one picker for each match.")

## Pick an official for a match

1. Open the division, then **Schedule**, then the **Officials** tab.
2. Find the match in the table. It lists the **Fixture**, its **Kick-off** and the **Officials** already on it.
3. Open the picker in the **Assign** column and choose a name.

The official goes on the match as its **referee**, whatever roles they hold. The picker holds one official per match: choosing another name replaces the first, and **— none —** clears the match. A match with no time yet can still have an official.

## Read their replies

Each assigned official shows a mark in front of their name:

- **✓** they accepted.
- **·** they have not replied.
- **✗** they declined. Hold the pointer over the chip to see **Reason:** and what they wrote.

A decline is a flag for you. Nothing is reassigned for you. Pick someone else yourself.

## Warnings

- **Blackout dates.** When an official has marked a date as one they can't make, the picker shows **— unavailable** after their name for matches on that day, and an assigned chip shows **⚠**. It is a warning only. You can still assign them.
- **Booked elsewhere.** When an official who works for more than one organisation has a match around the same time somewhere else, the picker shows **booked elsewhere** and the time. Only the time is shown. It never says which organisation, competition or match.
- **On the board.** On the [schedule board](/help/scheduling/board), a match on a court shows **umpire declined** when its official declined, and **umpire unavailable** when the official has a blackout on that day. A match that has no court yet shows neither.
- **On the match page.** Open a match and its **OFFICIALS** strip shows each official with their role and reply, for example **Marta Quinn**, **Referee**, **Accepted**.

> **Example**
> You pick Marta Quinn for two matches, at 20:23 and 20:33. She accepts the first and declines the second with the reason "Travelling that day", and she marks 19 November as a date she can't make. The tab now shows **✓ Marta Quinn referee ⚠** on the first match and **✗ Marta Quinn referee ⚠** on the second, with **Reason: Travelling that day** on hover. In every picker for that day her name reads **Marta Quinn — unavailable**. Nothing moves until you pick someone else.

## Propose

**Auto-assign** fills the officials in for you. You need Pro, an Enterprise plan, or an [Event Pass](/help/billing/event-pass) on the competition. On Community, **Propose** shows **Auto-assigning officials (solver, phased sourcing) is on Pro and the Event Pass — manual assignment still works.**

1. Choose the settings: **Stay on one court per block**, **Keep officials in their pool**, and **Fairness** by **whole tournament** or **per day**.
2. Select **Propose**. It places officials on matches that have a kick-off time. If none do, it says **Nothing to assign yet. Officials are placed onto fixtures that have a kick-off time — publish the schedule (Board tab) and add at least one official above, then Propose again.**
3. Select **Apply** and the number, for example **Apply 4 assignments**.

### What Apply does

Apply replaces every assignment in the division that is not locked, including the ones you picked by hand. An official keeps their reply only on a match where the same official and the same role come back. Everyone else starts at pending.

Propose can place an official on a date they marked off. The **⚠** appears afterwards, so check the chips after you apply.

> **Example**
> Marta Quinn is on five matches: three with a kick-off time and two with no time yet. She has accepted four of them. The division has four matches with a kick-off time. You select **Propose**, and the button reads **Apply 4 assignments**. After you apply, Marta's two assignments on matches with no time are gone. On Cedar Hill v Amber Ward she comes back as referee, so she keeps her reply: still accepted. On the two matches where Ola Brandt is now the referee, and on the match Marta is new to, the reply is pending.

## Emails

An official with an email address on record gets **New officiating assignment** when you assign them new matches, by hand or with Apply. They get **Match moved** when one of their matches changes time, court or venue. Applying an assignment that did not change sends nothing.
