You score chess by tapping the winner and, if you want, saying how the game ended. A draw has its own tile.

## Choose the time control

You pick a time control when you create the division: **Classical**, **Rapid** or **Blitz**. A new chess division starts on **Blitz**. In the division's rules you also set the clock family, the time per side, the increment and the move delay. For a knockout stage you set the tie-break games, in order: **Rapid**, **Blitz** or **Armageddon**. See [knockout deciders](/help/scoring/knockout-deciders).

## The board

Each half names a player and shows a dash until the game is decided. The result then shows as **1**, **½** or **0**. A dot beside the name marks the player with White (its label reads **Playing White**). The header says **Colours tracked** when the division tracks colours.

## The pairing card

**Pairing card** is a tile that appears before the match starts. It asks for the **Board number**, with a **−** and **+** stepper and **Confirm**.

## Record the result

1. Start the match.
2. Tap the half of the player who won. The half reads **Tap to record the winner**.
3. The pad records "Result recorded" and asks **How did it end?** with **Checkmate**, **Resignation**, **Flag fall**, **Adjudication** and **Illegal move**. Answering is optional, and the result stands without it. A countdown runs and the panel clears.

For a draw, press **Draw / no result**. It asks **How did it end?** with **Draw by agreement**, **Stalemate**, **Insufficient material**, **Adjudication**, **Threefold repetition**, **Fifty-move rule** and **Dead position**.

> **Example**
> Meena and Ravi agree a draw. You press **Draw / no result** and choose **Draw by agreement**. The pad shows ½ for each side.

One tap decides the game, so a wrong tap matters. Press **Take back** on the ribbon to undo it. See [fixing a mistake](/help/scoring/corrections).

## Online Chess: sizes and credits

Online Chess plays your division's games on Lichess, on a Seazn board. You pay for it once, before the first round, with [Seazn credits](/help/billing/match-credits). Players never see a price or a credit.

### Turn it on and choose a size

In **Division rules**, on the division's Fixtures tab, set **Online Chess** to **Lichess**. A **Players, up to** menu appears under it. The size is the most players who can enter, and it becomes the division's registration limit. A new online division opens at the smallest size that holds the players already entered.

Each size includes the Swiss rounds shown, so you do not count rounds yourself. The price is the same on every plan and for every time control: classical costs what rapid and blitz cost.

### Swiss and knockout only

Online Chess runs Swiss and knockout events, including play-offs, double elimination and tie-breaks. League, group and ladder formats cannot play online. The format picker marks them **Not available for Online Chess**, and a division that holds one cannot turn Online Chess on.

### Pay for it

Until it is paid, the Fixtures tab shows an amber line that starts **Online Chess not paid.** and says how many players are registered, the size, and that round 1 cannot open until it is paid. Its buttons are **Pay**, followed by the credits it will take, and **Change size**. **Pay** opens a receipt that asks first about the **Fair-play camera**: **Yes** or **No**, set to what the division's camera setting says now. The price follows your answer. Below it are the size, what comes from this month's credits and what comes from bought credits, and the total. Paying also sets **Camera during online play** in the division's settings to your answer: **Yes** sets it to **Required** unless the division already uses the camera, and **No** sets it to **Off**. With **Required**, players need a live camera to take their seat, and the receipt says so before you pay. If you are short, the receipt offers **Buy credits**, and comes back when you do.

You can also pay when you open the first round. Pressing **Open round** on an unpaid division opens the same receipt, titled **Pay and open round 1**. Pay it, and the round opens in the same step. If the round cannot open, for example because the division has not been started or a board in the round is not paired, nothing is charged and you see the reason. A board whose player has not linked Lichess does not stop the open: you pay, the round opens for the other boards, and that board waits. If no player has linked yet, the receipt says so, and you can open round 1 again for free once they link.

This month's included Seazn credits are spent first, then bought credits. Included credits that are not used by the end of the month expire. Bought credits never expire.

An unpaid division can still be started and scored by hand. Only opening an online round needs the payment.

### Change the size until round 1

Until a round of the division opens, you can change the size from the amber line, or from the line that shows the size once it is paid.

- Moving up charges the difference. Anyone on the waiting list is promoted into the room the new size makes.
- Moving down returns the difference. Bought credits come back in full. Included credits come back only if their month has not ended. The receipt shows what is returned and what is **Not returned**.
- Choosing **Yes** for the **Fair-play camera** on a package paid without it charges the difference. Choosing **No** on a package paid with it returns the difference, the same way as moving down, and the receipt shows what is **Not returned**.
- A change of size and of camera in the same receipt is one payment of the difference between the two.
- Turning the camera off in the division's settings instead returns nothing, and turning it back on there costs nothing more.
- The receipt always opens at the division's camera setting. If you turned the camera off in the division's settings after paying for it, the receipt opens at **No**, and paying it returns the camera's difference. To keep the camera instead, turn it back on in the division's settings.

Once any round of the division has opened, the size and the camera are fixed. Nothing is refunded for places you did not fill. Adding Swiss rounds after round 1 stays free.

### Refunds

A paid package is refunded automatically, with no button to press, when you delete the division or the competition, or switch Online Chess off, before any round has opened. Switching off asks **Switch off Online Chess?** and the answer is **Switch off and refund**. The credits you bought and this month's included credits go back to your balance, and the page says how many came back and how many included credits from an earlier month were not returned.

After a round has opened nothing comes back. Switching off then says **A round has opened, so this event is running. Switching off stops online play and no credits come back.**

Every payment, size change and refund appears in **Credit history** on the **Seazn credits** side of **Settings** > **Credits**, in rows that start **Online Chess:** and name the division and its size.

## Online chess: adding time to check-in

On a division that plays its chess online, players check in before each game. If a board is slow to fill, you can give it more time from [Match Day](/help/scheduling/match-day): on the board's own panel, on its row in the round panel's waiting list, or for the whole round from the round panel.

- **+5 min (3 left)** adds five minutes to that board's check-in. The number is how many extensions the board has left, and **Check-in closes** under it shows the deadline. After a press the panel answers, for example **Added 5 minutes to board 3.**, and gives the new closing time.
- **+5 min to the round** is in the round panel while the round is checking in. It does the same for every board of the round that is eligible at that moment.
- **Waiting on 2 boards** is the round panel's list of boards in an open check-in that still have a player to check in. Each row has its own **+5 min** button, and the answer shows under that row.
- It works only while check-in is open, from the moment a board's check-in opens until its deadline. Before it opens, or once it has closed, there is no button, and Match Day draws nothing in its place.
- Each board can be extended up to three times. After the last one the button is gone.
- The round button skips boards whose check-in has not opened yet or has already closed, boards whose game has started, and boards with no extensions left. It also skips any board you are playing on yourself. After a press the panel says how many boards it extended and why it left the rest, for example **Added 5 minutes to 6 boards.** and **2 boards skipped: game started**. When no board of the round can take more time, the round button is gone and the last answer stays on screen.
- Only organisers who can run the day get these buttons. A Viewer sees the round and its boards but no button, and a competition that went read-only after a [downgrade](/help/billing/downgrade) shows none either.
- Players don't need to do anything: the check-in countdown on their board updates to the new deadline.
- Every use is logged. Each board extended gets a row in the online-chess audit log, the same log that records a video override, with who pressed it, which board and the new deadline.

## The league table

When players are level, the division's **Standings** tab lists the order under "Tie-break cascade": points, direct encounter, wins, Sonneborn–Berger, then a drawing of lots.

## Recording level

A new chess match opens at **Key moments**, the finest level chess has. See [choosing a detail level](/help/scoring/fidelity).
