You score table tennis by tapping the half of the side that won the rally. The pad adds **Deuce** when it is needed and can follow the serve once it knows who served.

## Choose the format

You pick a format when you create the division. A new table tennis division starts on **Best of 5**. A game is won by two clear points, with no cap.

| Format | Match | A game is played to |
|---|---|---|
| **Best of 5** | Best of 5 games | 11 |
| **Best of 7** | Best of 7 games | 11 |
| **Hardbat (21 points)** | Best of 5 games | 21 |

## Score a rally

1. Open the match in the console and start it.
2. Tap the half of the side that won the rally. The half reads **Tap to award the rally**.
3. Keep tapping. When a game is won, the next game opens.

The line above the scores says which game you are in, for example "Best of 5 · Game 2". It adds **Deuce** when both sides are one point short of the target and level: 10–10 in Best of 5 and Best of 7, 20–20 in Hardbat. There is no cap, so play goes on until one side leads by two.

> **Example**
> A Best of 5 game is 10–10 and the pad shows **Deuce**. The home side wins the next rally and leads 11–10. The game is not over. The home side wins the next one again and the game closes 12–10.

In doubles the pad asks **Which player won it?** and offers both partners. The rally counts whether you answer or not. In singles it credits the side's one player. See [crediting the player](/help/scoring/attribution).

To undo a rally, press **Take back** on the ribbon. See [fixing a mistake](/help/scoring/corrections).

## The serve

The strip carries **Games** and **Serving**, then **1st serve** or **2nd serve**. A side serves two points in turn, then the other side does.

The pad does not guess who served first. **Note the server** is a tile of its own. Press it, say **Who served?**, then **Who won it?** for one rally. From then on the strip names the server and shows **1st serve** or **2nd serve**, and it alternates after every two rallies.

## Time-outs and sanctions

- **Time-out** is one tile per side. One tap records it, for example "Time-out recorded — Home".
- **Sanction** is one tile per side. It offers **Warning** and **Penalty**.

## The expedite system

**Start expedite** records the umpire starting the expedite system. It is offered at 0–0 and is hidden once it is running. It is also hidden when both sides have more than 9 points in the game, for example at 10–10. The strip then shows **Expedite**.

While expedite is on and the pad does not know who served, each rally offers a **13th return?** chip with a **13th return** button, for the receiver winning on the thirteenth return. The pad does not time the ten minutes. That part of the rule is yours to judge.

## Record a game's final score

**Set score** records a finished game as two numbers. It is offered while the current game has no rally in it, so it comes back when a new game opens.

## Recording level

A new table tennis match opens at **Every detail**. See [choosing a detail level](/help/scoring/fidelity).

## Questions

**Where are substitutions?** The table tennis pad has no substitution tile.

**How does the league table break ties?** The division's **Standings** tab lists the order under "Tie-break cascade": points, head-to-head, head-to-head set ratio, head-to-head point ratio, head-to-head among those still level, then a drawing of lots.
