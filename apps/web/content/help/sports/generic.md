Pick **Any other game** when your sport is not in the list, for example netball, squash or kabaddi. The pad knows two sides and a number, or two sides and a winner. It has no goals, rallies or periods.

## Choose the format

You pick a format when you create the division. A new division starts on **Score**.

- **Score**: a running tally you tap during play. Draws are allowed. The header reads "Running score · Draws allowed".
- **Win/loss**: a winner and nothing else. There are no draws. The header reads "Result only · No draws".

Under the two halves, a line gives the margin, for example "Home leads by 2".

## Score: tap during play

1. Tap a half. It reads **Tap to add a point**. One point goes to that side at once.
2. If the point was worth more, a panel asks **Worth more than 1?** with **2 points**, **3 points** and **5 points**. Pick one before the panel clears.

Your pick replaces the point you just recorded. It does not add a second one. A side with one named player credits that player.

> **Example**
> You tap Meena's half. She has 1. The panel asks **Worth more than 1?** and you press **3 points**. Meena has 3, not 4. If you then press **2 points** in the same window, she has 2.

## Finish a Score match

- **Finish from tally** records the running score as the result. The tile shows the score, for example "1 – 0".
- **Enter final score** asks **Home score**, then the away score, each with a **−** and **+** stepper and **Confirm**.
- **Correction** takes points off the tally.

You can score before pressing the start button. The pad accepts a tap on a fixture that has not started.

## Win/loss: one tap

Tap the half of the winner. The match is decided at once and the match page reads "decided" with "Meena A won" and **Finalize result**. There is no tally. **More** holds **Add points**.

## Recording level

A new match of **Any other game** opens at **Key moments**. See [choosing a detail level](/help/scoring/fidelity). To undo an entry, press **Take back** on the ribbon. See [fixing a mistake](/help/scoring/corrections).

## The league table

A win pays 3, a draw 1 and a loss 0. When sides are level, the division's **Standings** tab lists the order under "Tie-break cascade": points, difference, total scored, head-to-head, then a drawing of lots.
