You record an ice hockey penalty with the **Penalty** tile for a side. The pad asks for the class, the reason and the minutes. It then shows who is in the box and how long is left. This page covers penalties. For goals, periods and overtime see [ice hockey](/help/sports/icehockey).

## Record a penalty

1. Tap **Penalty** for the side. The pad asks **Which penalty?** and lists the classes in order of severity: **Minor**, **Bench minor**, **Double minor**, **Major**, **Misconduct**, **Game misconduct** and **Match penalty**.
2. Choose **What for?** The list runs from Elbowing, Charging and Boarding to Delay of game, Too many men, Fighting and Other.
3. Check the **Minutes**. The step opens at the class's usual length. Change it if the official gave more.
4. Choose **Served by** if a team-mate sits the penalty.
5. Choose **Who was penalised?**

A game misconduct lasts the rest of the match, so it has no minutes step.

| Class | Usual minutes |
|---|---|
| Minor | 2 |
| Bench minor | 2 |
| Double minor | 4 |
| Major | 5 |
| Misconduct | 10 |
| Match penalty | 5 |

## Recreational

The Recreational format offers only **Minor** and **Bench minor**.

## The box and the strength

After a minor penalty the strip shows **ON ICE** with the strength, for example **4V5**, and **BACK ON** with a countdown. A **Release** tile appears for taking a player out of the box. A misconduct or a game misconduct does not leave the team short, so the strength does not change for them.

A minor or a bench minor ends early when the other side scores.

> **Example**
> The home side takes a minor penalty in an IIHF match. The strip shows **ON ICE 4V5** and **BACK ON** counts down from 2 minutes. The away side scores on the power play after 1 minute. The minor ends there, and the strip goes back to full strength.

## Questions

**Where are cards?** Ice hockey penalties are classes, not cards. Field hockey has cards. See [field hockey](/help/sports/hockey).
