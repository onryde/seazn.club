A **device link** turns a phone or tablet into the scorer for exactly one match. The person holding it needs no account and installs nothing.

## Hand one over

1. Open the fixture and choose **Remote scoring**.
2. Press **Create scoring link**. You get a QR code and the link as text. It works at once.
3. Show it to whoever is at the court, or press **Copy link**.

The phone opens a card headed **Check the match before you start**, with both names. When they are the right players, the holder presses **Start match** and scores on the same pad you use.

Come back to the panel later and it reads "A scoring link is live for this match until it is over", with **Show QR**, **Revoke** and **Revoke & reissue**. **Show QR** shows the same code every time, so a QR you printed on a [scorer sheet](/help/scoring/scorer-sheets) keeps working.

## What the holder can do

- Score that one match, with the same pad, [detail levels](/help/scoring/fidelity) and [player credits](/help/scoring/attribution) you have.
- Void the link's own entries with the pad button **Void my last entry**, until the result moves the competition on. A link cannot void an organiser's entry or one made through a different link. See [fixing a mistake](/help/scoring/corrections).

What the holder cannot do:

- **Finalize.** The app answers "Finalizing needs an organiser or scorer account". A sport that records the result as a score (generic, for example) decides the match when the holder enters the final score.
- **Open another match.** A link for a different match is refused with "This device link is for a different fixture".
- **See or change anything else** in the competition.

## When a link stops working

A link works until the match is finalized or cancelled, or its result moves the competition on. There is no daily cut-off. It stops when:

- **The match is over.** Once it is finalized or cancelled, the phone shows the result with no controls.
- **The result moves the competition on.** In a knockout that is the moment the result is entered, because the winner goes straight into the next match. After that the link is refused with "This result has already moved the competition on", and corrections are the organiser's, from the console. In Swiss the same happens when the next round is paired, and in any format when the stage is completed.
- **You revoke it.** If a sheet is lost or the link reached the wrong person, choose **Revoke & reissue**: it switches the old link off and makes a new one. **Revoke** switches it off with no replacement. The old QR answers "This scoring link was revoked. Ask the organiser for a fresh link." on its next tap.
- **The match is deleted.** **Rebuild fixtures** deletes every match in the stage and makes new ones, so every link and printed QR for that stage stops working. The rebuild dialog warns you first. Deleting the stage, undoing the step that generated its matches, or a Swiss round losing a board when the number of players changes does the same to those matches' links.

> **Example**
> A knockout of four, generic scoring. You give Court 3's phone the link for the first semi-final. They enter 2-1, the match is decided, and the winner is placed in the final. They notice a mistake and try to void it: the link is refused. You open the match in the console and correct it there.

## Common questions

**Device link or official?** An [official](/help/scoring/scorer-role) has an account and can score every match they accept. A device link is one match and no setup, for the volunteer at Court 3.

**Two phones scanned the same QR.** Two phones that scanned the same QR share one link, so either can void the other's entries, and both can score. If they tap at the same moment both taps land. See [score conflicts](/help/scoring/conflicts). Keep to one phone for each match.

**What if the court has no signal?** The pad queues entries. See [scoring without signal](/help/scoring/offline-scoring).
