A **device link** turns any phone or tablet into a scoreboard for exactly one fixture — no account, no app install.

## Hand one over

On a fixture, choose **Hand over device**, then **Create scoring link**. You get a link (and QR) to hand to whoever is at the court. It works immediately.

Need it again? **Show QR** shows the same link every time, so a QR you already handed out or printed on a [scorer sheet](/help/scoring/scorer-sheets) keeps working.

## What the holder can do

Score that one match, with the same pad a signed-in scorer gets — [choosing a detail level](/help/scoring/fidelity), [crediting a player](/help/scoring/attribution), the lot. What they can't do: finalize the match, see other fixtures, or touch settings. If they enter something wrong, they can undo any of their own entries from the activity feed — never another device's or another scorer's — until the result moves the competition on. In a knockout, that is the moment the result is entered, because the winner goes straight into the next match; in Swiss, it is when the next round is paired; in any format, it is when the stage is completed. After that the device can't change the match any more, and corrections are the organiser's, from their console. See [fixing a mistake](/help/scoring/corrections) for how undo works.

## Lifetime and revocation

- A device link works until the match is finalized or cancelled. There is no daily cut-off.
- Handing the same match over again never replaces the link — you get the same QR.
- If a sheet is lost, or the link reached the wrong person, choose **Revoke & reissue**: the old QR stops working and you get a new one.
- **Revoke** turns the link off with no replacement — the device is signed out on its next tap.
- Deleting the match ends its link too. **Rebuild fixtures** deletes every match in the stage and makes new ones, so every link and printed QR for that stage stops working — the rebuild dialog warns you first. Deleting the stage, undoing the step that generated its matches, or a Swiss round losing a board when the number of players changes does the same to those matches' links.

## Common questions

**Scorer role vs device link?** Scorers are teammates with accounts and standing assignments across many matches. Device links are one match, zero setup — made for the parent volunteering at Court 3.

**What if the court has no signal?** Same as any other pad — entries queue on the device and send once it's back online. See [scoring without signal](/help/scoring/offline-scoring).
