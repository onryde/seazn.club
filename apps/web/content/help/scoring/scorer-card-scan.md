A [scorer sheet](/help/scoring/scorer-sheets) card is a QR for one match. This page is for the umpire holding it, and for the organiser who wants to know what the umpire will see.

## Scan and score

1. Scan the card's QR with the phone's camera. The match opens in the browser.
2. The phone shows **Check the match before you start**, with the court, the time, the match and both names. If these are the players in front of you, press **Start match**.
3. Score the match on the pad. A match that has already started opens straight on the pad.

One phone can score a whole court. Scan each card as its match comes up. Each scan opens only that match.

## When the phone shows something else

- **Not started yet:** the organiser has not started this division, and the screen moves on by itself once the division starts.
- **Waiting for** both sides. A side is not decided yet. The screen says "This page updates by itself." and moves on when the result is in. There is no need to scan again.
- **The final score with no controls.** The match is over. The phone says why, for example "Match over — result finalised. Ask the organiser to correct it."
- **This scoring link was revoked.** or **This scoring link is not valid.** The card no longer works.

The names on a card can be out of date. Trust the phone, which shows who is in the match now.

## When a card stops working

A card works until its match is over. There is no daily cut-off, so cards printed the night before are fine. It stops when:

- **The match is over.** Once it is finalized or cancelled, scanning shows the result with no controls.
- **The result moves the competition on:** the card then shows the result with no controls too. In a knockout that is the moment the winner goes into the next match, in Swiss it is when the next round is paired, and in any format it is when the stage is completed. After that the corrections are the organiser's, from the console.
- **The organiser chooses Revoke & reissue.** The old QR stops working at once. Print the day again, or use **Show QR** on the match, for the new one. **Revoke** on its own switches the link off and makes no new one.
- **The match is deleted.** **Rebuild fixtures** deletes every match in the stage and makes new ones, so every card for that stage stops working. Deleting the stage does the same, and so does undoing the step that generated its matches. A Swiss round that loses a board because the number of players changed before it was paired does it for that board. Print again after any of these.

> **Example**
> The umpire at Court 2 scans the 10:30 card. The phone shows **Check the match before you start** with "Sana Ito" and "Jonas Lindqvist". They are the players on court, so the umpire presses **Start match** and scores. Later the organiser loses a card and chooses **Revoke & reissue**. The lost card now answers "This scoring link was revoked", and the next time you print the day, it carries a new card. Cards already printed keep the old code.

## Common questions

**Two phones scanned the same card.** Two phones that scanned the same QR share one link, so either can void the other's entries, and both can score that match. If they tap at the same moment, both entries land. See [score conflicts](/help/scoring/conflicts). Keep to one phone for each match.
