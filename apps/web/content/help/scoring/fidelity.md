Every pad has a **Recording** chip above the scoring actions. It shows the level you are on in words. Tap it and a sheet lists the four levels, each with the number of actions the pad gives you at that level, so you can see what a level buys before you pick it.

![The Recording sheet on a football pad: Result only 3 actions, Key moments 3, Full timeline 11, Every detail 12 with a tick](/help/scoring/recording-sheet.webp "The Recording sheet on a football pad with a lineup saved. Every detail is the level in use.")

## The four levels

- **Result only.** The final score and the outcome.
- **Key moments.** The headline events, enough for a scoreboard or a match card.
- **Full timeline.** A full, ordered account of the match with who did what. [Player stats](/help/players/player-stats-and-photo) are usually built from this.
- **Every detail.** The finest detail the sport offers.

A higher level never shows fewer actions than a lower one, and it does not always show more. Some sports have nothing extra to record at a level, and then two levels offer the same actions. The sheet shows you the counts, so you can see that before you pick.

> **Example**
> On a live football match with a lineup saved, the sheet in the picture shows 3 actions at **Result only**, 3 at **Key moments**, 11 at **Full timeline** and 12 at **Every detail**. With no lineup saved the same match shows 3, 3, 10 and 11. On a live match with no lineup saved, badminton offers 1, 3, 3 and 5: **Key moments** and **Full timeline** are the same. Generic scoring offers 1, 3, 3 and 3, so only **Result only** differs. Cricket offers 1, 7, 7 and 9. The numbers can change with a sport's rules and with the state of the match, for example before it starts.

## Every level is on every plan

All four levels are available on every plan, including Community. The chip names no plan and nothing on it is locked. Pick the level that matches how closely you can follow the match.

## Changing level during a match

Change it whenever you like. It applies straight away.

- **Up:** more actions appear on the board from that point on.
- **Down:** the deeper actions leave the board. They are removed, not greyed out, so the pad only shows what a tap will record.
- Nothing already recorded is deleted either way, and going back up brings the actions straight back.

## The level belongs to your pad

The level you pick applies to your own screen. Another scorer, or a [device link](/help/scoring/device-links) at the other end of the court, can be on a different level at the same time.

A match you have never picked a level for opens at the finest level its sport has. For some sports that is **Key moments** (generic, carrom and chess), because they have nothing finer.

## Common questions

**Does a low level mean worse stats?** It means fewer. **Result only** still gives a correct final score and correct standings.

**An action I used a minute ago is gone.** You are on a lower level than the action needs. Open **Recording** and pick a higher one.
