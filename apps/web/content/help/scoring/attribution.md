Many entries can say **who** as well as **what**: a goal's scorer, who took a card, a wicket's fielder. That is crediting a player. It puts the entry on a person's record as well as their side's.

## Where the names come from

The names come from the saved [lineup](/help/scoring/lineups): starters and bench, for the side the entry belongs to. Coaches and staff on the team sheet are never offered. Set the team sheet before the match so the names are there to pick.

## How it looks in football

A football goal is recorded the moment you press **Goal Home** or **Goal Away**. The pad then opens a **Goal detail** strip with **Own goal**, **Penalty** and the names of the side's players. The strip clears by itself after a few moments. Tap a name to credit the goal, or ignore the strip: the goal is already in and the score has already changed.

Other entries open a sheet with the names inside it, and some sheets mark a name as **Required** when the entry cannot be recorded without one. In football, **More**, then **Shot**, is one of them: **Confirm** stays disabled until you pick the player.

> **Example**
> Hawks score. You press **Goal Home** and the score goes to 1-0. In the **Goal detail** strip you tap **Hawks Player C**. If you had ignored the strip, the goal would still count for Hawks and for the score, with no scorer on it.

## Skipping it

Where a name is not marked **Required**, leaving it blank is fine. The entry still counts toward the score, the standings and the activity feed. What is missing is the player detail: an entry with no name cannot be placed on a player's record.

With no lineup saved, the strip has no names to offer and only shows **Own goal** and **Penalty**.

## Common questions

**I picked the wrong player.** There is no edit in place. [Void the entry](/help/scoring/corrections) and record it again with the right name.

**Does crediting a player change the score?** No. The score comes from what happened. The name is an extra layer on top.
