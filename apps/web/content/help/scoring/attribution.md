Many scoring actions ask **who** as well as **what** — a goal's scorer, a wicket's fielder, who took a card. That's attribution: crediting an event to an actual person instead of leaving it as just "something happened to this side."

## Where the names come from

Attribution picks from your saved [lineup](/help/scoring/lineups) — starters and bench, either side. Set the team sheet before the match so the full roster is there to pick from; without one, the pad says **No roster available yet.** instead of guessing.

Only players are offered. A coach or other staff member on the team sheet never appears as a scoring candidate — see [roles](/help/scoring/lineups).

## Skipping it

Attribution is never required to record an action. Tap a name to credit it, tap it again to clear it, or just leave it blank and confirm — the event still records and still counts toward the score, standings and the [activity feed](/help/scoring/corrections). What's missing is the player-level detail: without a name, that event doesn't add to anyone's personal stats.

## Common questions

**I picked the wrong player — can I fix it?** [Undo the entry](/help/scoring/corrections) from the activity feed and record it again with the right name; there's no way to edit attribution on an entry in place.

**Does attribution affect the score?** No. The score comes from what happened — a goal, a point, a wicket. Who gets credited is a separate, optional layer on top of it.
