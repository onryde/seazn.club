An **official** is someone from your officials pool, such as a referee or umpire, who is assigned to a match and has accepted. They sign in with their own account, score that match, and cannot see the rest of your competition. There is no separate "scorer" role on your team any more. Team roles are **owner**, **admin** and **viewer**, and an official is not on your team.

## Set one up

1. Open **Directory**, then **Officials**, and add the person to the pool.
2. Press **Invite**, enter their email and press **Send invite**. If the email does not arrive, share the claim link shown below the form. It is shown once.
3. They open the link and claim the profile. They now have an account.
4. Assign them to a match on a division's schedule.
5. They accept the assignment. Until they do, the match is not theirs: the app refuses their entries with "You are not a member of this organization".

## What an official sees

They open **My matches & teams**. Each assigned match is listed with its role (for example **As Referee**), the competition, the status (**Accepted**) and **Score this match**. An official with no assignments sees "No matches assigned to you yet."

They are not a member of your organisation. The app refuses them everywhere else: another match in the same competition, the competition's page and its data all answer as if they were not there.

## What an official can do

On a match they have accepted:

- **Start** the match, enter points and results, at any detail level.
- **Void** their own entries or anyone's on that match, until it is finalized. See [fixing a mistake](/help/scoring/corrections).
- **Finalize** the match.
- **Enter lineups** for it.

They cannot void anything after the match is finalized. The app answers "This fixture is finalized — ask an organiser to reopen it". They also cannot decline an assignment they have already accepted.

> **Example**
> You assign Priya Nair to the match between Ana and Ben as referee. She claims her profile, accepts, and scores it from **My matches & teams**. When she has finished she finalizes it. Ben's team says the score is wrong. Priya cannot void it any more, so you correct it from the console.

## Common questions

**Do officials use a seat on my team?** No. They are not on your team, and the number of team members your plan allows does not count them.

**Can I give someone one match for one day?** Use a [device link](/help/scoring/device-links). It needs no account and no assignment.
