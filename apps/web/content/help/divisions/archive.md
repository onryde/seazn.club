You can archive a division to hide it, or delete one that never started. Both live in the **Danger zone** on the division's **Settings** tab.

## Archive

**Archive division** hides the division from your console and the public site. Nothing is destroyed. A confirm box asks you to choose **Archive division** again. You do not type anything.

Archive is refused while registration is open for the division. The message says to close registration first, then archive it.

To bring a division back, open the competition's **Settings** tab and choose the **Archived** tab, which shows a count in brackets. The list shows each archived division with **Restore** and **Purge…**, and says restore brings everything back exactly as it was. Restoring checks your plan's division limit again, so it can be refused when the competition is full.

![The Archived divisions list in a competition's settings, with Restore and Purge buttons on each row](/help/divisions/archived-divisions.webp "Archived divisions, each with Restore and Purge")

## Archive and your division limit

Whether an archived division still counts depends on whether it has results.

- **No results yet.** Archiving gives the place back to your limit. If you set a division up wrong, archive it and start again.
- **Results recorded.** The division keeps its place in your limit, archived or not.

The Danger zone tells you before you click. On a division with results it shows "This division has recorded results, so archiving it will not free a division slot." The confirm box says the same and adds that support can waive the slot if you archived it by mistake.

When your plan's limit refuses a new division, the new-division builder says "Adding another division needs a bigger plan" and adds that divisions with recorded results count toward your limit even after they are archived.

The limit for divisions in one competition is {{plan:divisions.per_competition.max:community}} on Community and {{plan:divisions.per_competition.max:pro}} on Pro, with no limit on Enterprise.

> **Example**
> A Community competition already has {{plan:divisions.per_competition.max:community}} divisions. You archive one that was never started, and you can create a new one straight away. You archive one that played matches, and creating a new one is still refused.

## Delete

**Delete division…** removes a division for good. It is only possible **before the division starts**. Once a division has started, delete is refused, even when nothing has been scored. The message says to archive it instead. You confirm a delete by typing the division's name.

Delete destroys the division, its stages, fixtures, schedules and entrant entries. People, teams and clubs are kept, because they belong to your organisation.

You can purge an archived division permanently. **Purge…** unlocks after a 30-day wait. Until then the button is greyed and its hint names the date.

Registration must be closed before you delete, as it must before you archive.

Delete is also refused while card payments for entries in the division are not fully refunded. The message says to refund them first, or to archive the division instead. Archive stays available when delete is refused for this reason. Refunds are covered in [refunds and disputes](/help/registration/refunds-and-disputes#refunds).
