Every division has a **Settings** tab beside Entrants, Fixtures, Standings and Stats. People who can edit the competition see it. This page lists what is on it.

![A division's Settings tab: General, a note pointing to Division rules, Required court tags, News auto-drafts, Public page, Sharing and embed, and the Danger zone](/help/divisions/settings-tab.webp "The Settings tab of a division that has not started")

- **General.** Rename the division with **Save name**. Upload a **Card logo** with **Upload image**. Without one, the card wears a monogram.
- **Required court tags.** Court tags the division requires. It reads "Any court" until you set some.
- **News auto-drafts.** A section that reads Off until you change it. See [news](/help/sharing/news).
- **Public page.** Holds options for the division's public page, such as whether seed numbers show.
- **Sharing & embed.** Widgets for your website. See [embeds](/help/sharing/embeds).
- **Danger zone.** **Archive division** and **Delete division…**. See [archive and delete](/help/divisions/archive).

Some sports add sections. A chess division also has **Online play** and **Camera**.

## The format and the rules are not here

A note on the tab says so: the format, the match rules, the points and the tie-breaks are set on the **Fixtures** tab, under **Division rules**. Choose **Open Division rules** to jump there.

- **Format** changes the competition structure. See [a division's life](/help/divisions/lifecycle) for when it locks.
- **Match rules**, **Points** and **Tie-breaks** on the **Division rules** card apply to every stage. A stage card has its own **Match rules**, **Points** and **Tie-breaks** for that stage alone.

Once a stage has started, its card shows "Locked: the stage has started" beside its rules.

The kinds of entrant a division accepts are on the **Entrants** tab. See [teams, individuals and pairs](/help/entrants/kinds).

> **Example**
> You run a league and a knockout in one division. The **Division rules** card says every stage plays its rules unless the stage card sets its own. To give only the knockout different match rules, open **Match rules** on the knockout's stage card and change them there.
