A merge keeps the record you merged in. It is marked as absorbed and hidden from your roster, with a copy of everything it held. That is what makes **Undo** possible. An undo works as well next month as it does thirty seconds later.

![The Recent merges panel at phone width: the line Undo puts both records back exactly as they were, and one entry reading Ines Okonkwo absorbed ines OKONKWO with an Undo button.](/help/players/recent-merges.webp "Recent merges. Undo sits on an entry that has not been undone.")

## Undo a merge

1. Open **Directory**, then the **Players** tab.
2. Find **Recent merges**. Owners and admins see it. Each entry reads a name, **absorbed**, and the other name. Newest is first, and the list shows the newest 50.
3. Choose **Undo** on the entry.
4. The window reads **Undo this merge?** and says the merged-in record comes back as its own record and the kept record returns to the settings it had before. Choose **Undo the merge**.

The entry then reads **Undone** and has no **Undo** button. The two records are separate again. If their names still match, they are back in [Possible duplicates](/help/players/find-duplicates), and you can merge them again.

## What an undo puts back

- Both records, each with its own name, date of birth, reference and photo.
- The merged-in record's account link, if it had one.
- The kept record's public settings as they were before. Under the stricter-setting rule, the merge may have hidden something the kept record showed.
- Squad entries, lineup places, availability answers and bans, each on the record that held it.
- Statistics, counted again for the two records.

## What an undo leaves alone

An undo puts back what the merge moved. It leaves a rename and a team you added afterwards alone.

- **A later edit stays.** If you renamed the kept player after the merge, the new name stays.
- **A later team stays.** A team you added the kept player to after the merge stays on the kept record.
- **An edit to something the merge moved is replaced.** If you changed a squad number the merge had carried over, the undo puts back the numbers from before the merge.
- **Public settings are replaced too.** The kept record's public name and photo settings go back to what they were before the merge. If the player turned one off after the merge and it was on before the merge, the undo turns it back on, so check the settings after an undo.

## Limits

- **One undo per merge.** A second undo of the same entry is refused. The entry stays in the list, marked **Undone**.
- **Newest first.** If you merged A into B and then B into C, the second merge has to be undone first. Choosing **Undo** on the first merge is refused with a message that tells you to undo the later merge first.

> **Example**
> **Leila Haddad** is on file twice. The record you keep has 1 goal. The record you merge in is linked to a login, is captain of **Riverside Rockets** with #9 and has 2 goals. You merge them. The kept record now shows 3 goals, the login and #9. You then change the squad number to 12 and rename the kept player **Leila Haddad-Noor**. You choose **Undo** on the entry. The merged-in record is back with 2 goals, its login and #9. The kept record is back with 1 goal and no squad number. It is still named **Leila Haddad-Noor**.
