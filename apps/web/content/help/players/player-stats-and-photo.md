Your numbers come from the matches you play. There is nothing to enter. Play, get scored, and the numbers appear on your **Player home**. This page covers where you see them, what is public, and your photo.

![A player home at phone width: the Career card for football with one division, one variant and one match, Goals and Points; My stats for the division; a Public profile link, and the What's public about you card with the name and photo tickboxes.](/help/players/stats-privacy.webp "Career, My stats and the privacy card on a player home. The Public profile link shows once the name box is ticked.")

## Your stats

- **Career** has one card for each sport you have been scored in. Each card adds up every division in that sport, for example **1 division · 1 variant · 1 match**, with the sport's own numbers such as **Goals** and **Points**. Before your first scored match it reads **No career stats yet — they'll appear here once you've played in a scored match.**
- **My stats** has one block for each division you were scored in, with the same numbers for that division. It includes private competitions, because the numbers are yours.

The labels come from the sport, so a footballer sees goals and another sport shows its own. Both sections are there on every plan, because they are your own numbers and no organisation's plan applies to them.

The organiser's **Stats** tab on a division lists every player with the sport's numbers, sortable by each one. Members of the organisation see it, whatever their role, on every plan.

Where [plans at a glance](/help/billing/plans) and the [Event Pass](/help/billing/event-pass) list **career stats** as something Pro or a pass adds, they mean the organisation's rollup of one player across its competitions, not your **Career** card. The organiser reads that rollup through the API. It needs Pro, or an Event Pass for the competition the matches were played in. Community does not get it. Your **Career** card does not depend on it.

## What is public about you

**What's public about you** has a card for each club that has you on file. The screen says: **These switches control whether your name and photo appear on public pages — per club.** Each is a tickbox:

- **Show my name publicly**
- **Show my photo publicly**, which appears once you are on a team

A change can take a few minutes to reach public pages.

## Your public player card

A public player card shows your name, your matches and your stats in that competition, and your team and squad number. Three things must all be true before one exists:

1. **Show my name publicly** is ticked. Nothing the club pays for turns it on.
2. The club's plan carries public player cards. Pro and Enterprise do, and so does a competition with an [Event Pass](/help/billing/event-pass). Community does not. See [plans at a glance](/help/billing/plans).
3. The division shows full names. A youth division shows a first name and an initial by default, so it has no card.

When all three hold, **Public profile** appears beside your block in **My stats** and opens the card. When one fails, there is no link. The card's own page then reads **This player page isn’t available**.

> **Example**
> **Dana Whitfield** has scored 1 goal in **Premier** at a Pro club, so **My stats** shows **Goals 1** and **Points 1**. Her name tickbox is off, so there is no **Public profile** link. She ticks **Show my name publicly**. The link appears beside her block, and the card shows Dana Whitfield, **Riverside Rockets** and **#9**. Public pages can take a few minutes to catch up. At a Community club the same goal shows in **My stats** and the tickbox works, but there is no link and the card page is not available.

## Your photo

Once you are on a team, a photo control sits above the tickboxes. Choose **Add photo** to upload your own. You do not need to ask an organiser, and it works on every plan. A photo shows publicly only when **Show my photo publicly** is ticked and a public card exists.

## Under 16

If the organiser has your date of birth on file and you are under 16, an organiser manages your public name and photo settings until your 16th birthday. The tickboxes are locked, with the line **An organiser manages this until you're 16.** You cannot upload a photo. Without a date of birth on file there is no lock.
