The same player often ends up in your directory twice: typed in one season, imported the next, registered by a club and again by themselves. The **Possible duplicates** panel finds those pairs for you. It sits in **Directory**, on the **Players** tab, under the heading **Duplicate check**.

![The Possible duplicates panel at phone width: a To review count, then a pair card for Ines Okonkwo with Same name and Same date of birth lit and Plays for the same team dim, and a Review button.](/help/players/duplicates-panel.webp "A pair card. The chips under Match strength show why the two records match.")

## What you see

Each pair is a card with both records and a **Match strength** row of three chips: **Same name**, **Same date of birth** and **Plays for the same team**. A chip is lit when it applies. A **To review** count shows how many pairs there are. Pairs with more lit chips come first.

Choose **Review** to [merge the pair](/help/players/merge-players). Nothing changes until you do.

The list is worked out each time you open the page. Nothing is stored. Fix a pair, by merging it or by correcting a name, and it is gone the next time you open the page. When there is nothing to show, the panel reads **No duplicates to review**, **Every player in this directory has a record of their own.** and **Checked live, every time you open this page.**

## What makes a pair

Two records need the same name. Capitals and extra spaces do not count: "ines   OKONKWO" matches "Ines Okonkwo". Accents do count: "Zoë Hart" and "Zoe Hart" are not a pair until you make the names match.

A shared date of birth and a shared team raise the strength. A pair where one record has a date of birth and the other has none is still listed. Three records with one name give three pairs. Review them one at a time.

## What the list leaves out

- **Two different dates of birth.** Two dates are the clearest sign of two people who share a name. You can still [merge them by hand](/help/players/merge-players#merge-by-hand).
- **Two different accounts.** If two different logins claimed the two records, they are two people.
- **A player and an official.** The two are never the same record.
- **A record that was merged away.** It is no longer on your roster.

The panel shows up to 50 pairs. Merge or correct some, then open the page again to see more.

## Who sees it

Owners and admins see **Review** on each card. A viewer sees the pairs and their chips but no **Review**, and the **Players** list has no actions column. Someone who is not a member of the organisation gets a page not found. The check works the same on every plan.

> **Example**
> You imported a spreadsheet and typed one player by hand. **Ines Okonkwo** was born on 2 April 2001 and "ines   OKONKWO" has the same date. The panel lists them first, with **Same name** and **Same date of birth** lit. **Tomas Brandt** appears twice on **Riverside Rockets**. That pair is next, with **Same name** and **Plays for the same team** lit, and the team shown. **Mira Solheim** appears twice with birth years 2000 and 2001. That pair is not listed, because the dates differ. **Joss Parr** appears twice, once with a date of birth and once with none. That pair is listed with only **Same name** lit.
