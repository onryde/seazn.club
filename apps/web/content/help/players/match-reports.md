If you officiated a match, you can file a **match report** afterwards: what happened, and a row for each card, injury or other incident the organiser should know about. Filing is free on every plan.

![The Match report form on a player home: a What happened box, one incident row with Kind set to Red card, a player picked from a list and a note, then Add incident and Submit report.](/help/players/report-form.webp "The report form, with one incident row filled in.")

## Where to file

You need a claimed officiating profile. See [claim your player profile](/help/players/claim-your-profile) and [officials](/help/scheduling/officials).

1. Open your **Player home**. The officiating section lists **Completed matches**, with the number in brackets. Open it.
2. Choose the button on the match. It reads **File match report**. Once you have saved a draft it reads **Finish match report**. Once you have filed it reads **Match report filed**.

**Completed matches** lists your finished matches, cancelled ones included. Only a match that is decided, finalised or abandoned, and that you **accepted**, gets the button. A match you did not accept has no button.

## Write it

- **What happened** is a short summary.
- **Incidents**: choose **Add incident** for each one. Pick the **Kind**: **Red card**, **Misconduct**, **Injury** or **Other**. Optionally pick the **Player (optional)** from either team. Write a **Note**. Every incident needs a note: **Add a note to each incident, or remove it.** Choose **Remove** to drop a row.

Your work saves as a draft when you leave a box, and **Draft saved** appears. You can close the form and come back with **Finish match report**.

## Submit it

1. Choose **Submit report**.
2. The form asks **Submit report? You can't edit it after.** Choose **Submit report** again, or **Cancel** to go back.
3. The form is replaced by **Report filed**, with the time, your summary and each incident.

A filed report is final. If something needs changing, ask the organiser.

## What the organiser gets

- **An email.** Every owner and admin of the organisation is emailed. Viewers are not.
- **Where it shows.** Every report opens from **Schedule**, then **Officials**, where a link such as **Mark Ref's report** opens it. **View report** on the **Discipline** tab appears only on a suspension the report raised, so only where the sport has cards and the plan has enforced discipline. Viewers do not see the **Discipline** tab.
- **A suggested ban.** A **Red card** or **Misconduct** incident that names a player creates a pending suspension, tagged **From match report**, when the club's plan has enforced discipline. Pro, Enterprise and an Event Pass have it. Community does not. The suspension does not need discipline rules to be switched on for the division. Nothing is banned until the organiser chooses **Confirm** or **Waive** on the **Discipline** tab.

Reports are not shown on public pages.

> **Example**
> You refereed **Rovers** against **City**, and the match is decided. **Completed matches (3)** lists it with **File match report**. You write "Tense derby, one sending-off." and add one incident: **Red card**, **Sent Off Player · Rovers**, note "violent conduct in the 88th". **Draft saved** appears, and the button now reads **Finish match report**. You submit. The club's owner and admin are emailed. On a Pro club, the **Discipline** tab shows **Sent Off Player** under **Pending review** with **From match report**, **Matches 1** and a **View report** link. On a Community club the same report is filed and no suspension appears.
