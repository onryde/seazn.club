Once a player has [claimed a profile](/help/players/claim-your-profile), their player home lists their matches. The player answers whether they can play. The organiser sees the answer next to each name on the lineup. At the venue, a player scans a QR code to show they have arrived. This page covers both sides.

![A player home at phone width: the Next match card with the two teams, the date and time, a Match page link, and the In, Maybe and Out buttons with a note box.](/help/players/player-home.webp "Next match on a player home. A note box opens once an answer is picked.")

## Your player home

Your **Player home** opens after you claim a profile, and when you sign in as a player with no organisation of your own. If you also run an organisation, choose **Player home** in the menu. It shows:

- **Next match** is the first match still to play, with the competition, the division, the date and time, and a **Match page** link. Later matches sit under **Coming up**.
- **In**, **Maybe** and **Out** sit under each match to come. Tap one to answer. A note box, **Add a note for the organiser (optional)**, lets you explain. Your answer and note save when you tap and when you leave the box. Tap a different answer later to change it. The note stays.
- **Recent results** lists your finished matches with the score. A finished match has no answer buttons.
- **My teams** lists your teams, each with its division and organisation.

Before anything is scheduled, the page reads **You're on a team — matches will appear here once your organiser schedules them.** If you are on no team it reads **Nothing here yet. When an organiser rosters you into a team or draw, your matches land on this page.**

## Check in at the venue

An organiser puts a QR code up at the venue. You scan it with your phone.

1. The page reads **Match check-in**. If you are signed out, sign in first. There is no password.
2. Tap **I’m here — check in**.
3. The page reads **You're checked in** and **The organiser can see you're here. Good luck!**

Your player home now shows **Checked in** beside your answer. If you had answered **Maybe** or **Out**, your answer stays and the check-in is added. If you had not answered, checking in marks you **In**.

Check-in works for a claimed player on one of the match's teams. Anyone else, including a claimed player who is not on this match, sees **Claim your profile first**. An unclaimed player needs the organiser to send a [claim invite](/help/players/invite-to-claim).

## For organisers

**Answers on the lineup.** Open the match, then **Lineup**. A chip sits beside each name: **✓** for available, **✗** for unavailable, **?** for maybe available. A dash means no answer yet, or a player who has not claimed a profile. Point at a chip to read the player's note. A small lime dot beside a chip means the player checked in at the venue.

**The check-in code.** On the match page, **Check-in QR** opens **Player check-in** with a QR code and a link you can copy. Print it or pin it at the venue. The button shows while the match is waiting to start.

The window says the code stops working at midnight local time. That is midnight at the end of the day you create it, not the day of the match. Create it on match day. A code created on Sunday stops on Sunday night, even for a match on Monday. Local time is the division's time zone, or your organisation's if the division has none.

Scanning keeps working while the match is on. When the match is finished, check-in is closed.

> **Example**
> **Riverside Rockets** play **Dunmore Park** tomorrow at 02:26. The lineup shows **Dana Whitfield** with **✓** and the note "Back from work at 6", and **Eli Marsh** with a dash because he has not claimed his profile. You create the code today at 00:27. It stops at midnight tonight, which is before the match, so you create it again tomorrow. Dana scans it, and a lime dot appears beside her chip. Her answer is still **In**.
