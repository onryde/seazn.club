Mexicano is americano with a memory. You still change partner every round, but the next round is not fixed in advance. Once every match of a round has a result, the players are ranked by their points and the next round is drawn from that ranking. Players on similar points end up on the same court, so the matches stay close all evening.

## Where your points come from

Your points are the points your pair scored. In the example, {{fact:mexicano-next-round.roundOneFirstMatch}} ended {{fact:mexicano-next-round.roundOneFirstScore}}, so {{fact:mexicano-next-round.leader}} and {{fact:mexicano-next-round.leaderPartnerOne}} both have {{fact:mexicano-next-round.leaderPoints}}. Partners always share the total from the match they played together, and the totals grow round by round.

## How the next round is drawn

Seazn lines the players up from the most points to the fewest and cuts the line into groups of four, one group for each court. The top four play on court 1. Inside each group, the first and the fourth play against the second and the third, so in the example court 1 is {{fact:mexicano-next-round.topCourtMatch}} and the last court is {{fact:mexicano-next-round.bottomCourtMatch}}.

Pairing the first with the fourth is the point of the format. The two leaders, who just won together, are split up and play against each other, and each of them takes a partner from lower in the group. Each match has one strong and one weaker player on each side, so the stronger players cannot simply stay together and pull away.

## Round one, and players level on points

Before round one nobody has any points, so there is nothing to rank. Seazn draws round one in a fixed order of its own, not by the seeds you set. In the example the players are listed from Ana to Hal, and round one still opens with {{fact:mexicano-next-round.roundOneFirstMatch}}. The same order decides between players who are level on points later on, which is why two partners on the same total are listed in one order or the other.

## Which sports can play it

Mexicano is offered in the same sports as americano. The ranking needs a score for each side of every match, and Seazn reads that score only when the match is scored as any other game, where you type both sides' scores at the end.

## What does not work yet

Two parts of mexicano are not finished, and both matter if you plan an evening around it. On the division page, Seazn draws round one, but once round one is scored the page offers to complete the stage and has no way to draw round two. And scores are entered properly only in a division of any other game: in badminton, table tennis, tennis and carrom a score typed in the boxes is refused, and a match scored on the pad leaves every player on 0 points. Until both are fixed, an americano of any other game, whose rounds are all drawn at the start, is the format to run.
