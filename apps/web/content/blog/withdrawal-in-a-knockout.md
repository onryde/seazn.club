When a player leaves a knockout, Seazn does not redraw the bracket. The player they were due to meet goes through by walkover, and every result already played stays as it was.

{{example:knockout-withdrawal}}

## Why the opponent goes through

In the example, {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.withdrawn}} won a quarter-final and then withdrew before the semi-final against {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.opponent}}. The quarter-finals were played, so they stand: {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.playedResults}} results, unchanged. Only the match {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.withdrawn}} had still to play changes, and it becomes a walkover for {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.opponent}}.

A bracket is a set of paths drawn before play starts. Bringing back the player {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.withdrawn}} beat would reward a loss, and redrawing would change the path of every player who did nothing wrong. Giving the walkover to the opponent changes one match and leaves every other path as it was drawn.

## What the walkover shows

The match is marked as forfeited, with a note that a withdrawal was recorded, and it shows the walkover score of your sport where your sport has one, as any forfeit would. The table and the bracket treat it as a win for the opponent, who moves on to the next round. This is the same in every sport: in all {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.sportCount}} the opponent wins the match and goes on.

## When the next opponent is not known yet

A player can also withdraw before their next opponent is known. Say {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.withdrawn}} has won a quarter-final, and the quarter-final beside it, which feeds the same semi-final, is still to be played. The semi-final waits for that match: whoever wins it walks over {{fact:knockout-withdrawal.withdrawn}} and goes straight through.

## When both players have gone

If the player due to receive the walkover has also left, there is nobody to send on. The match becomes a double walkover, and the player waiting in the next round gets the walkover instead. A final has no next round, so when both finalists have gone, the title is left vacant.
