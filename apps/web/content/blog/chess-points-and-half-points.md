Chess scores a win **{{fact:chess-points.halfWin}}**, a draw **{{fact:chess-points.halfDraw}}** and a loss **{{fact:chess-points.halfLoss}}**. A chess league in Seazn also offers a second way to count: **{{fact:chess-points.threeWin}}** for a win, **{{fact:chess-points.threeDraw}}** for a draw and **{{fact:chess-points.threeLoss}}** for a loss, as in football. The same games can put the players in a different order under the two.

{{example:chess-points}}

## Reading the table

Ana wins some of her games and loses the rest. Ben draws every game he plays. Under the usual chess count Ana has **{{fact:chess-points.anaHalf}}** and Ben has **{{fact:chess-points.benHalf}}**, so {{fact:chess-points.halfLeader}} leads, because a draw is worth half a win and Ben drew every game. Under the football-style count Ana has **{{fact:chess-points.anaThree}}** and Ben has **{{fact:chess-points.benThree}}**, and {{fact:chess-points.threeLeader}} leads: wins pay so much more than draws that Ana's wins outweigh Ben's draws.

Neither count is wrong. They answer different questions: the half-point count rewards a player who never loses, and the football-style count rewards a player who wins. That is why an organiser chooses one before the stage starts, and why it is worth telling the players which.

## What changes from sport to sport

- **Chess.** A chess league offers {{fact:chess-points.leaguePresets}} presets, the half-point count and the football-style count. A Swiss tournament offers {{fact:chess-points.swissPresets}}, the half-point count.
- **Every other sport.** Only {{fact:chess-points.halfPointSports}} of the {{fact:chess-points.sportCount}} sports, chess, counts in half points. {{fact:chess-points.wholePointSportsList}} count whole points.

## Where you set it

On a chess stage's card in the division's Fixtures tab, **Points** shows the two counts as presets, and a stage that has not started can switch between them. The picture shows the league editor with the half-point preset chosen.
