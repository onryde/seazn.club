A finished match pays each side some points, and the numbers depend on the sport. Some sports pay a loser nothing, and some pay a loser a point. A few sports also rank their table by matches won instead of points. The table below was made by playing real matches in all {{fact:standings-points.sportCount}} sports.

{{example:standings-points}}

## Reading the table

Each sport's first row is for the variant a new division of that sport starts on. When another variant of the same sport pays differently, it gets a row of its own with the variant in brackets. Start with football: a win earns **{{fact:standings-points.footballWin}}** points, a draw earns each side **{{fact:standings-points.footballDraw}}**, and a loss earns **{{fact:standings-points.footballLoss}}**.

The last column says what the table is sorted by first. That is points in most sports, and matches won in volleyball, badminton and tennis. Where a sport cannot end a match with nobody winning, the third column says so.

## What changes from sport to sport

- **Football and hockey.** A win earns {{fact:standings-points.footballWin}} in football and {{fact:standings-points.hockeyWin}} in FIH outdoor hockey. A draw earns each side {{fact:standings-points.footballDraw}} in football and {{fact:standings-points.hockeyDraw}} in FIH outdoor hockey. In the FIH variant with a shoot-out a match cannot end level: the shoot-out winner earns {{fact:standings-points.hockeyFihshootoutWinSo}} and the loser {{fact:standings-points.hockeyFihshootoutLossSo}}.
- **Cricket.** In T20 a win earns {{fact:standings-points.cricketWin}}, a tie earns each side {{fact:standings-points.cricketTie}}, and a match with no result earns each side {{fact:standings-points.cricketNoResult}}. A Test match can also end in a draw, which earns each side {{fact:standings-points.cricketTestDraw}}.
- **Chess.** A win earns {{fact:standings-points.boardgameWin}}, a draw earns each side {{fact:standings-points.boardgameDraw}}, and a loss earns {{fact:standings-points.boardgameLoss}}.
- **Volleyball.** A new division starts on beach volleyball, where a 2-0 win earns {{fact:standings-points.volleyballWin20}} and a 2-1 win earns {{fact:standings-points.volleyballWin21}}, and the loser earns {{fact:standings-points.volleyballLoss20}} and {{fact:standings-points.volleyballLoss21}}. In indoor volleyball the score of the match decides the points: a 3-0 win earns the winner {{fact:standings-points.volleyballIndoorWin30}} and the loser {{fact:standings-points.volleyballIndoorLoss30}}, and a 3-2 win earns the winner {{fact:standings-points.volleyballIndoorWin32}} and the loser {{fact:standings-points.volleyballIndoorLoss32}}.
- **Table tennis.** A played win earns {{fact:standings-points.tabletennisWin30}} at 3-0 and {{fact:standings-points.tabletennisWin32}} at 3-2, and a played loss earns {{fact:standings-points.tabletennisLoss30}} at 3-0 and {{fact:standings-points.tabletennisLoss32}} at 3-2.
- **Ice hockey.** In the IIHF variant a win in regulation earns {{fact:standings-points.icehockeyWin}} and a loss earns {{fact:standings-points.icehockeyLoss}}. A win in overtime or a shoot-out earns {{fact:standings-points.icehockeyWinOt}} and the loser earns {{fact:standings-points.icehockeyLossOt}}. In recreational ice hockey a win earns {{fact:standings-points.icehockeyRecreationalWin}}, a loss earns {{fact:standings-points.icehockeyRecreationalLoss}}, and a draw earns each side {{fact:standings-points.icehockeyRecreationalDraw}}.
- **Badminton, tennis and carrom.** A win earns {{fact:standings-points.badmintonWin20}} in badminton, {{fact:standings-points.tennisWin}} in tennis and {{fact:standings-points.carromWin}} in carrom. A loss earns {{fact:standings-points.badmintonLoss20}} in badminton, {{fact:standings-points.tennisLoss}} in tennis and {{fact:standings-points.carromLoss}} in carrom.
- **A generic division.** A new division starts on the score variant: a win earns {{fact:standings-points.genericWin}}, a draw earns each side {{fact:standings-points.genericDraw}}, and a loss earns {{fact:standings-points.genericLoss}}. The win/loss variant pays {{fact:standings-points.genericWinlossWin}} for a win and {{fact:standings-points.genericWinlossLoss}} for a loss, and has no level result.

Points are one part of a ranking. The last column of the table shows what each sport sorts by first: {{fact:standings-points.footballRankedBy}} in football, and {{fact:standings-points.volleyballRankedBy}} in volleyball, {{fact:standings-points.badmintonRankedBy}} in badminton and {{fact:standings-points.tennisRankedBy}} in tennis.
