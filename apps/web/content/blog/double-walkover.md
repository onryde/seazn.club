Sometimes nobody comes. A double walkover is a match that neither side turned up for. Seazn books it as a loss for both sides, gives neither side anything, and keeps it out of every ratio the table uses to separate teams.

{{example:double-walkover}}

## Why both sides lose

The match was on the schedule and neither side played it, so the table counts it as played and lost by both. That keeps the played column honest: everyone can see a match was due and was not played. Neither side earns a point, and nothing goes into goals, sets, games or runs, so the match cannot help or hurt anyone's goal difference or ratio.

Because nothing is credited, the match page and the stream overlays show no score for it: the match page reads "{{fact:double-walkover.sentence}}", and in a knockout it adds who gets the walkover next. Where a short label fits, such as the printed bracket and the slideshow, the tag {{fact:double-walkover.tag}} stands in for the score.

It is not the same as each side losing a played match. In table tennis a played loss still pays {{fact:double-walkover.tabletennisPlayedLoss}} in the table, but a double walkover pays {{fact:double-walkover.tabletennisPoints}}, the same as any walkover loss ({{fact:double-walkover.tabletennisWalkoverLoss}}). Nobody is rewarded for not turning up.

## In a knockout

In a knockout a match has to send someone on, and here there is nobody to send. So nobody goes through, and the player waiting in the next round gets a walkover instead. This is the same in all {{fact:double-walkover.sportCount}} sports.

That is also why you cannot enter "neither side turned up" as the result of a match that feeds no next match: a final, a third-place match, or the last step of a stepladder. Someone has to be left standing there, so Seazn does not offer "neither" on those matches. Reschedule it, or record a walkover if one side did turn up.

A withdrawal is the one way a final can still end this way. If both finalists have withdrawn, the final is booked as a double walkover, and nobody wins the title: it is left vacant, and the final's tile reads that way. The withdrawal post for knockouts tells that case. A third-place match is booked the same way when both of its players have gone, and it leaves the title alone.

## On a ladder

A ladder has no next match to feed, so a double walkover is accepted there on any challenge. It leaves both players where they were, as the ladder post shows.
