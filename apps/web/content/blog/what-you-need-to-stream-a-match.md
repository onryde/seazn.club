You need four things: **an Android phone, a tripod that holds it sideways, power for the whole match, and a steady upload connection.** No laptop, capture card or computer streaming software.

{{art:stream-flow}}

| What | Minimum | Better |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone | Android 12 or later, with the Seazn Club Mobile App | A spare phone that nobody needs during the match |
| Tripod | Holds the phone sideways and stays put | Tall enough to see over people standing in front |
| Power | A full battery and a charger | A power bank that stays connected while you stream |
| Internet | 4G, 5G or Wi-Fi that holds steady at the spot where the tripod stands | Tested at that spot before match day |
| Seazn | A Seazn credit, and your YouTube stream key or a connected YouTube channel | Both set up the day before |

## The phone

Streaming uses the Seazn Club Mobile App on Android 12 or later. It does not stream from an iPhone.

Use a phone that can stay on the tripod: once it is filming, you do not want someone walking off with it to take a call. If a call is answered on that phone, the stream loses its sound until the call ends. A video call can also freeze the picture.

Before match day, clear some of the usual interruptions:

- Turn on Do Not Disturb, and check its settings let no calls through. Some settings still let favourite contacts ring.
- Allow the app to run in the background without battery restrictions. The app's stream settings show whether it is allowed.

## The tripod

The stream is filmed with the phone **sideways**, so the holder must grip it that way. Check three things:

- **It does not wobble.** A shaky tripod can mean a shaky picture, so try it on the ground where it will stand.
- **It is high enough.** Stand it where the whole court or pitch fits, above the heads of people watching.
- **It is out of the way.** Keep the legs away from the run-off and from walkways.

## Power

A long match drains the battery. Plug the phone in, or fix a power bank to the tripod.

- Charge the phone and the power bank the night before.
- Bring a cable long enough to reach without pulling on the phone.
- Check the cable while you set up: the phone should show that it is charging.
- In sun, shade the phone: a phone that gets too hot can slow down or turn itself off.

## Strong Wi-Fi or mobile data

What matters is **upload** speed, at the place where the tripod stands, while people are there.

- The app sends 720p video at 30 frames a second. The video starts at 1.5 Mbps and rises as your connection allows, **up to 3 Mbps**. On a weak connection the rate drops.
- Sound and other data go with the video, so at full quality the app expects to upload more than that: about 3.6 Mbps. By our calculation from that figure, not a measurement, full quality uses **about 1.6 GB of data an hour**, or about 5 GB for a three-hour match.
- Run a speed test on the phone at the tripod spot. To leave room above 3.6 Mbps, we suggest an upload of **at least 5 Mbps** that stays there.
- Venue Wi-Fi is shared with everyone connected to it, so we suggest testing it again once people have arrived. If you are not sure of it, use mobile data.
- On mobile data, check your plan's allowance. If the camera phone uses another phone's hotspot, check that plan too: some plans limit hotspot data separately.

## Set up in Seazn first

1. Add your YouTube stream key. In YouTube Studio, select **Go live**, then **Stream**, and copy the stream key. In Seazn, open **Streaming** and select **Add streaming key**. An owner or admin adds it once, and you can then pick it for any match.
   The other option is to select **Connect YouTube** on the same page and connect your channel. A phone that follows a court all day needs a connected channel: a court cannot use a stream key.
2. Make sure you have a Seazn credit. Each video uses one. One credit covers one match stream, up to 3 hours: see [Seazn credits](/help/billing/match-credits).
3. For one match, open the match, select **Stream** and pick the destination. In the Seazn Club Mobile App, open **Live Stream**, scan the match's code, then select **Go live**.
4. For a court, select **Stream this court** on Match Day, pick the connected channel, and scan the court's code the same way. Then switch on the matches to stream.

## Five-minute check at the venue

- Phone charged, and charging from the cable
- Phone sideways, whole playing area in the picture
- Tripod steady, legs out of the way
- Speed test done at that spot
- Do Not Disturb on
- Code scanned, and the app shows it is ready
- Players and parents know the match is being filmed, and you have the permissions your club requires
