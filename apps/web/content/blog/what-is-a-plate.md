In a knockout, half the field goes home after one match. A plate is a second knockout for exactly those players: everyone who loses their first match plays on in the plate, while the main draw carries on. "First match" means the first one a player actually plays, so a player with a bye goes into the plate if they lose in round two. A player who loses their first match by walkover did not play it, and does not go into the plate.

{{example:what-is-a-plate}}

## Why the plate takes first losses, not round-one losses

The plate exists so that everyone gets more than one match. A round-one loser has played once. A top seed with a bye who loses in round two has also played once. If the plate only took round-one losers, that seed would go home after a single match, which is the outcome the plate is there to prevent.

So the plate counts matches, not rounds. In the example the top seed, {{fact:what-is-a-plate.byeHolder}}, has a bye, loses their first real match in round two and joins the plate. A player who wins their first match and loses later has already had more than one match, so they stay out of the plate.

## Why a walkover does not count

A walkover is a match nobody played. A player who did not turn up for round one is usually not there for a plate either, and drawing them in would hand a plate opponent another walkover. So a first match lost by walkover, by withdrawal or by retirement is left out. In the example, {{fact:what-is-a-plate.excludedNames}} does not turn up and is left out. The opponent who goes through on that walkover has not played yet either, so their first match is their next one.

The plate in the example holds {{fact:what-is-a-plate.plateSize}} players: {{fact:what-is-a-plate.plateNames}}.

## Why the plate waits for the first matches

Who is in the plate is only known once every player has played their first match. Most play it in round one, but a player with a bye, or one who went through on a walkover, plays it in round two. So the plate's draw waits for those matches, and never for the main draw's final. Its size is whatever the field turns out to be.
