In a single knockout, one bad match ends your event. Double elimination gives every player a second life: you are out only after your second defeat. A first defeat moves you from the winners bracket to the losers bracket, and the two bracket champions meet in the grand final. A grand final reset keeps the promise in the final too, so the player who has never lost must also be beaten twice.

{{example:double-elimination-explained}}

## The two brackets

Everyone starts in the winners bracket, which works like an ordinary knockout. A player who loses there is not out. They drop into the losers bracket and keep playing. A player who loses in the losers bracket has now lost twice, and is out.

The losers bracket takes in the players dropping out of each winners round as they arrive, and its survivors play each other in between. Its champion has lost once. The winners bracket champion has not lost at all. They meet in the grand final.

## Why the grand final can need a reset

The grand final is not an even contest of records. The winners bracket champion arrives unbeaten, and the losers bracket champion arrives with one defeat.

- If the winners bracket champion wins, it is over: the other player has their second defeat.
- If the losers bracket champion wins, both players now have one defeat. Ending there would knock out a player after a single loss, which is exactly what double elimination promises not to do. So the two play again: the reset. Whoever wins it is the champion.

## Where to find it in Seazn

Pick **Double elimination** as the division's format. The bracket shows the winners bracket, the losers bracket and the grand final, and a player's second defeat puts them out. If the reset is on and the winners bracket champion wins the first grand final, the reset is not needed and is left unplayed.

Today a new double elimination is set up without the reset, so a defeat in the grand final is final. The reset is not yet a setting you can switch on in the division builder.

The draw does not depend on the sport, and double elimination is offered in all {{fact:double-elimination-explained.sportCount}} sports.
