Yes, for as long as the league is running, whether or not a match has been played. A league compares every team on the matches each has played against all the others, so a team that joins late must meet every team that is already in. Seazn does not redraw the rounds that are there. It adds the matches the newcomer owes, and leaves everything already played as it is.

## One match against each team

{{fact:joining-a-league-late.newcomer}} owe one match against each of the {{fact:joining-a-league-late.teams}} teams, and none against themselves. That is what every other team in the league has, one against each of the others, so the table can compare them with the newcomer on the same footing.

## The rest rounds that have not started

A team that rests in a round has nothing to play in it, so a round it rests in has room for one more match. A round counts as started as soon as one of its matches has. Where that round has not started yet, {{fact:joining-a-league-late.newcomer}} plays that team in it. Here that is {{fact:joining-a-league-late.restOpponents}}, in rounds {{fact:joining-a-league-late.restRounds}}. Nobody plays twice in a round, and no round gets longer for the teams already in the league.

## New rounds after the last

The other teams rested in rounds that have already started, so there is no room left for them. Their matches go in new rounds after round {{fact:joining-a-league-late.lastRound}}: rounds {{fact:joining-a-league-late.newRounds}}, with one match in each. The league now runs to round {{fact:joining-a-league-late.lastRoundAfter}}. A plain "one more round" would not be enough: {{fact:joining-a-league-late.appended}} of the {{fact:joining-a-league-late.owed}} matches could not fit in a rest round.

No round that has started is touched, and no result changes. The newcomer has {{fact:joining-a-league-late.homeGames}} home games and {{fact:joining-a-league-late.awayGames}} away.

## What the organiser is told

After the team is added, Seazn says how many matches it owes and in which rounds: "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.noticeCatchUp}}" It also says what is left to do: "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.noticeUnscheduled}}" And it says what it does not do: "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.noticeOpponents}}"

## A team that arrives by itself, and a league that is over

A team added by the organiser is placed at once, as above. A team that arrives some other way, such as an import or a registration, is not placed by Seazn. It waits in a strip headed "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.waitingTitle}}", with a button, "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.addToTable}}", that places it the same way and shows the same three lines. If no match has started yet, the strip also offers another way: "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.rebuildOffered}}" The "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.rebuildButton}}" button next to it does that.

Once the league is complete, the entrants page has no box to add a team in. It says: "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.lockedNote}}" A team sent in anyway is refused: "{{fact:joining-a-league-late.finishedRefusal}}" A league whose last match has only just been played is not complete yet, and still takes a team. What happens when a team leaves instead is in [what happens to a team's results when it withdraws from a league](/blog/withdrawal-in-a-league).

## In every sport

Leagues are offered in all {{fact:joining-a-league-late.sportCount}} sports, and the matches a late team owes are worked out from the schedule alone. The sport changes how each match is played and scored, and nothing about where a late team's matches go.
