Seeds are spread through a knockout so the best players can meet only late: seed 1 and seed 2 only in the final, the top four only from the semi-finals. A bracket always has a power-of-two number of lines, so when the field is smaller, some lines stay empty. Those empty lines are byes, and they go to the top seeds.

## Why spread the seeds

A knockout throws a player out after one loss. If the two best players met in round one, one of them would be gone before the event got going, and the final would be weaker for it. Seeding is the knockout's way of saving the strongest matches for last. It also rewards a good season or a high rating with an easier first match: in round one, the best seed plays the weakest player, the second-best plays the second-weakest, and so on.

## Why the byes go to the top

{{fact:knockout-seeds.fieldSize}} players do not fill a bracket evenly, so the bracket has {{fact:knockout-seeds.bracketSize}} lines and {{fact:knockout-seeds.byeCount}} of them are empty. A player whose line faces an empty one goes straight to round two. That is an advantage, so it goes where an advantage is earned: to the top seeds, here {{fact:knockout-seeds.byeHolders}}.

## Where each seed goes

The lines are filled so that, at every round, the top seeds are as far apart as they can be. In a {{fact:knockout-seeds.largeSize}}-line bracket the seeds sit in this order, from the top line down:

{{fact:knockout-seeds.lineOrder16}}

Read it in pairs and each first-round match adds up to the same number: the strongest seed against the weakest. Seed 1 is at the top and seed 2 at the bottom, in opposite halves. The top four are in four different quarters, so none of them can meet another before the semi-finals.

## Players without a seed

You do not have to seed everyone. The seeded players take their places first, and the rest are placed by a draw. The draw is fixed for the stage, so building the same bracket again gives the same result, and the order people signed up in never decides a place. A knockout that takes its players from an earlier stage is not drawn at all: it keeps the order that stage gave it.

## In every sport

Seeding works the same way in every sport: the knockout format is offered in all {{fact:knockout-seeds.sportCount}} sports, and the bracket never looks at the sport to place anyone.
