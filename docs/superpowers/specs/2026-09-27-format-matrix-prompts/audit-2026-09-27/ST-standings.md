## 0. Architecture in one paragraph

- **Engine owns the maths.** It folds per-fixture `StandingsDelta` pairs (`packages/engine/src/competition/standings.ts`) and ranks them by partition refinement over a `TiebreakerKey[]` cascade (`tiebreakers.ts:rankStandings`). An optional stage `PointsRule` re-derives points (`points.ts:applyPointsRule`). Rank locks are applied last (`points.ts:applyRankLocks`).
- **Web runs the engine and caches the result.** `apps/web/src/server/engine-db/competition.ts` loads fixtures and calls `completeTableStage` per pool. `recomputeStandings` writes `standings_snapshots`; `usecases/scoring.ts:789` triggers it for league/group/swiss only.
- **Every surface renders the snapshot.** Column choice is `lib/public-site.ts:226 standingsColumns`, fed by the module's `metrics` and the engine's `DERIVED_METRICS` (`display.ts:22`).
- **The cascade is per division, not per stage.** It is `divisions.tiebreakers ?? module.defaultTiebreakers` (`competition.ts:392`).
- **Points come from three places:**
  - the sport cfg (division-level, frozen per fixture since V347);
  - an optional per-stage `stages.config.points` PointsRule;
  - per-stage match rules (#804), which are sets-sports only and are barred from carrying points (`competition.ts:280-301`).

## 4. What is solid (no gap found)

- **Fold and ratio maths.** The fold is order-independent, and ratio comparisons are integer cross-multiplied.
- **Absent metrics.** An absent metric sorts as "no data" and is not treated as zero (#429).
- **Frozen config.** Per-fixture config snapshots stop a later points change from rewriting played matches (V347).
- **Cricket aliases.** The cricket `for`/`diff` alias order is fixed (`tiebreakers.ts` DIFF_KEYS/FOR_KEYS lead with gd/run_diff), so the #862 P1 fix has landed.
- **Qualification status.** The engine rule is sound and brute-force tested, and the web builder fails closed on every unreadable input (`qualification-view.ts:296-420`).
- **Departed entrants.** The division page, console and embed mark withdrawn/DQ entrants (`standings-table.tsx:63-66`).
- **Table-header i18n.** It is complete across 4 locales and enforced by a test.
- **Phone layout.** The division page and hub tables are verified at 320 px in e2e.
