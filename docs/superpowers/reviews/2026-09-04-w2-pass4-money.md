### I1 · Important · `apps/web/src/server/usecases/registrations.ts:118-120`
**`applicationFeeCents`' clamp survives its own deletion — proven by mutation.**

I replaced the body with `return Math.round((feeCents * percent) / 100);` and
ran `registrations.test.ts -t "fee math"`: **2 passed, 0 failed** — including
the test literally titled "never exceeds the fee itself". The reason is
arithmetic: both of its cases use percent = 100, where
`Math.round(f * 100 / 100) === f`, so `Math.min(f, f)` is an identity. The other
four cases use percent = 5, three orders of magnitude below where the clamp
binds. No test anywhere passes a percent above 100
(`registrations.test.ts:210-218, 5583, 5771` are the complete set of call sites).

The clamp is load-bearing.
`apps/web/src/app/api/admin/orgs/[id]/entitlement-override/route.ts:10` writes
`int_value: z.number().int()` with **no upper bound**, and `feePercentFor`
returns an override verbatim. A staff typo of `150` for `15` makes
`applicationFeeCents(2000, 150)` return 3000 on a £20.00 entry; Stripe rejects
the session with `application_fee_amount` greater than the amount, and every
registration checkout in that org 500s until someone finds the override row.

**Fix:** `expect(applicationFeeCents(2000, 150)).toBe(2000)` — one line, and it
kills the mutant. Optionally bound `int_value` in the override schema.

Mutation reverted: `shasum apps/web/src/server/usecases/registrations.ts` =
`82425dfd82722a1901e0cba793625d9ac56fef3a`, identical to the pre-mutation

### I4 · Important · `apps/web/src/app/o/[orgSlug]/c/[compSlug]/registration/page.tsx:130`
**The organiser's own fee panel quotes the LIVE plan rate while the charge uses
the LOCKED rate — and V398 just made the two differ for every pre-existing
community competition.**

`page.tsx:130` resolves `feePercentFor(auth.orgId, id)` and hands it to
`registration-hub-config-panel.tsx:1125`, which renders `reg.hub.config.feeCut`
as `{ keep: 100 - pct, pct }`. The CHARGE uses `effectiveFeePercentFor`
(`registrations.ts:107-114`, called at `registrations.ts:2353`), which returns
the frozen `competitions.fee_percent` whenever it is set.

entry before V398 stays locked at **8** while the panel now says **5**.

**Failure scenario with numbers:** a £20.00 entry in such a competition. The
panel tells the organiser they keep 95% (£19.00). `applicationFeeCents(2000, 8)`

life of the competition, against a number the product showed them.

this was only reachable after a plan change; V398 makes it the default state of
every pre-existing community competition.

**Fix:** the panel should read `effectiveFeePercentFor(id, orgId)`, and ideally
say the rate is locked when it is.

### I5 · Important · `apps/web/src/lib/currency.ts` (`creditPackOptions`) + `apps/web/src/components/buy-credits.tsx:144-146`
**The Buy Credits bonus badge is USD-derived and overstates the bonus in EUR.**

`creditPackOptions` computes `base` from `packs[0].credits /
packs[0].price.unit_amount` — the USD rate — and returns that `bonusPct`
alongside `amountFor(p.price, currency)`, the LOCAL amount.
`buy-credits.tsx:146` renders it as `"+{pct}%"` beside the local price.

Real bonus over each market's own baseline rung:

| pack | advertised | usd | eur | gbp | inr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| credits_25 | +5% | 5.0 | **2.7** | 5.0 | 4.8 |
| credits_50 | +10% | 10.0 | **7.6** | 10.0 | 9.8 |
| credits_100 | +15% | 15.0 | **12.5** | 16.5 | 14.7 |

A euro buyer is told "+15%" on the €92.00 / 460-credit rung when their real
advantage over the €9.00 / 40-credit rung is 12.5% — €92 buys 409 credits at the
baseline rate, not the 400 the badge implies. `credit-packs.test.ts:57-64` only
ever calls `creditPackOptions("usd")`, so nothing in the suite can see it.

The doc comment defends the USD derivation as "currency-stable, not a rounding
artefact of each currency's set price points". Stability is not the property
being advertised: the badge is a claim about *that customer's* money.

**Fix:** compute `bonusPct` from the requested currency's own baseline rung, and
loop `SUPPORTED_CURRENCIES` in `credit-packs.test.ts`.

### m4 · Minor · `apps/web/src/lib/platform-settings.ts:44-51`

latent hazard rather than a live defect. The live row is `5` (jsonb). Fix:
`typeof row?.value === "number"` before the bounds check.
