### I3 — `pricing.pass.crossover`, all four locales — a money comparison whose one-month assumption is not stated

String (en, `marketing.json:85`): `Up to about {amount} of entry fees a month, this is the cheaper option; above that it is Pro at {pro}/mo — a {proFee}% platform fee against {passFee}%.`
Renders as: "Up to about $150 of entry fees a month, this is the cheaper option; above that it is Pro at $14.99/mo — a 2% platform fee against 4%."

`lib/pricing-crossover.ts` solves `pass + F·passFee = proMonthly + F·proFee` for **one
month** of Pro; the module header and `CrossoverInputs.proMonthlyMinor` both say so. The
rendered sentence does not, and the pass is sold for a competition that runs for its
whole lifetime — the page's own subhead calls it "One-time Event Pass for the yearly
tournament."

Worked from the same seed and matrix: a competition running 3 months at $300/month of
entries costs **$47.99 on the pass** (1199 + 0.04·90000) against **$62.97 on Pro**
(3·1499 + 0.02·90000). The line tells that buyer Pro is cheaper. The true crossover is
`74950 − 59950/n` minor: $150 at one month, $449.75 at two, $549.66 at three — the
rendered figure is the most Pro-favourable point on the whole curve, so the bias is
systematic and always in the same direction.

Fix: state the assumption in the string ("for a competition that runs about a month…"),
or drop the sentence. The derivation itself is sound and worth keeping.

### I5 — `apps/web/src/lib/feature-copy.ts:32-36` — a scorer-seat refusal now names the wrong pool
