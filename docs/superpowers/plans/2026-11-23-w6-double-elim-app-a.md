### The rules stated once

- **Line names.** The rulebook's `LB a.b` is the stored `ext_key` `lb-r{a−1}-i{b−1}`; `WB a.b` is `wb-r{a−1}-i{b−1}`; GF1 is `gf`; the reset is `gf-reset` (engine `src/scheduling/bracket.ts:459-530` at d1f07f8, pinned 2026-11-23). Stored `round_no`: WB round r is `r`; LB round a is `2k + a` where `k` = WB rounds (`S/generators.ts:187-196` `bracketToGen`: `laneDepth + f.round + 1`, the engine's LB `round` already `k + L`; pinned 2026-11-23). So **`round_no = 1` is winners'-bracket round 1 and nothing else.**
- **Draw-dead seats (RB6-B1, B2, B3, E6; ruling 305(c)).** Base: a WB round-1 line drawn as a bye has no loser. Step: a seat fed by the **loser** of a line that has no loser is dead; a seat fed by the **winner** of a line with two dead seats is dead. Repeat until nothing changes. 0 dead seats = a match, 1 = a draw bye, 2 = draw-dead (void, never a double walkover). The rulebook's sets on a frame of 8: n=5 → `LB 1.1` (1), `LB 1.2` (2), `LB 2.2` (1); n=6 → `LB 1.1` (1), `LB 1.2` (1); n=7 → `LB 1.1` (1). n=3 → `LB 1.1` (1) (RB6-E4). A power of two, and n=2 → empty.
- **The draw mark.** `{ key: "bracket.slot.bye", params: { from: "draw" } }` on the dead seat. The plain bye label `{ key: "bracket.slot.bye", params: {} }` (`lib/bye-line.ts:12`, pinned 2026-11-23) keeps meaning a structural bye's phantom side or a **departure**. One helper spells the mark for its two readers (the resolver, `fixtureAwaitsSeedDraw`).
- **Lane role (RB6-P1, P2).** `<pool>:<lane>:<rounds before that lane's own final>:<seq_in_round>` for WB and LB; `<pool>:gf` and `<pool>:gf-reset` fixed. The lane is read from `ext_key`; a lane's final round is the highest `round_no` in that lane.
- **Late entry (RB6-L1 to L8, B6; rulings 263, 271, 296, 304; call 25).** Entry stage only; WB round 1 only; organiser's tap only. Case (1) a WB round-1 bye whose holder has not started WB round 2 **and** no entrant who advanced by bye through an LB line the reopened bye changes has started their next LB match (the lowest-seeded holder's when several); case (2) a withdrawn entrant's WB round-1 slot before its first match; case (3) the named refusal `KO_LATE_ENTRY_NO_SLOT`, with Rebuild offered when nothing has started and the schedule is not frozen.

## Review Focus

1. **A draw-dead seat whose partner seat later dies through departure.** A reasonable organiser expects a line nobody can ever play to be void, not a recorded double walkover naming two absent players. Pinned in Task 4: `W6 a draw-marked seat beside a departed loser's stamped seat is void, never a double walkover`.
2. **Two late players in a row into a field of 6** (two byes): the second tap must take the second bye and re-work the LB again, never land on a line the first already reshaped. Pinned in Task 9: `W6 late entry: two late players at n=6 take both byes`.
3. **A scorer starts the bye holder's next winners'-bracket match between the organiser's tap and the write, or a second organiser taps at the same moment.** The placer must wait for the scorer and then read the bye as closed — never half-reopen it; two taps must place two players on two byes or refuse one by name, never deadlock. Pinned in Task 9: `W6 late entry: a WB next match that starts while the placer waits closes case (1)` and `W6 late entry: two taps at once place both or refuse one by name, and never deadlock`. (Retargeted in the fix round: on 10.0.x the losers'-bracket match cannot start first — see Task 9.)
4. **A league holding an organiser's No result, auto-progress on.** The No result no longer blocks: the **next decided result** that finishes the stage advances it. A No result recorded as the last open match does not itself advance anything — the organiser presses Complete, as today (fix-round narrowing; no new trigger). Pinned in Task 6: `W6 open count: a league advances on its last decided result over an earlier No result` and `W6 open count: a No result recorded last leaves the stage running, and Complete succeeds`.
5. **A fed double elimination whose field is not a power of two, before and after the draw is confirmed.** The desk's red draw row must clear at the confirm and the dead lines must never take a court. Pinned in Task 5: `W6 fed draw: stageOwesDraw is false after the confirm`.
